# Review guide for bansuri teachers

Thank you for reviewing this course. It was written from published sources and checked for
internal consistency, but **no bansuri teacher has played through it yet**. Your review is the most
valuable improvement it can get. You do not need to use GitHub or read code: a list of notes is enough.

## What to look for

- **Wrong:** a fingering, note, raga rule or fact that is incorrect.
- **Unsafe or harmful habit:** advice that would build tension, poor embouchure or pain.
- **Order:** something taught too early or too late.
- **Missing:** something a student needs that is not taught.
- **Unclear:** instructions a beginner would misunderstand.
- **Tradition:** a point where your gharana or teacher teaches differently. The course marks such points as "Disputed" and gives both views. Tell us yours and its source.

## How to send your notes

Any of these:

1. **A GitHub issue** using the "Content correction" form in this repository.
2. **A document or email** with: file name, section heading, what is wrong, and what it should say.
3. **Voice notes or video**, if you prefer to demonstrate.

## Recording reference audio (very welcome)

Openly licensed bansuri recordings are very rare, so the app's reference sounds are amateur.
Short, clean recordings by a teacher would help every learner:

- **Sa** held for 5 seconds, then the octave pair `S` and `S'`, then the low notes `.N .D .P` (each 3 seconds).
- **The scales:** Bhupali, Kalyan and Bilawal, up and down, slowly.
- **The first melodies** in [EX-MEL](bansuri-mastery/exercises/EX-MEL-practice-melodies.md), Levels 1-3.
- **Ornament demonstrations:** one meend, one kan, one murki, slow then at normal speed.

Record in a quiet room, phone about 50 cm away, no effects. Say which flute key you used. To be
included, recordings need an open licence: CC BY 4.0 (credit to you) or CC0 (public domain). Send
them with the "Reference recording" form in this repository, or attach them to an email. Your name
will appear in the credits unless you choose CC0 without credit.
