## 1. Learning objectives

- Feel a steady **pulse** (beat) and clap with a metronome (RHY-01).
- Understand **tempo** (BPM) and the Indian **laya** terms (RHY-02).
- Clap and then play notes lasting 2 beats, 1 beat, 1/2 beat and 1/4 beat (RHY-03).
- Keep counting while playing without rushing or slowing (RHY-04).

## 2. Prerequisites

A metronome app ([L00](L00-day-0-your-flute.md)).

## 3. Simple explanation

### Pulse

When you tap your foot to a song, you are tapping its **pulse**, or **beat**: a steady, regular tick that runs under the music. Melodies are built on top of the pulse.

### Tempo

**Tempo** is how fast the pulse goes, measured in **BPM** (beats per minute). 60 BPM = one beat per second, like a clock. 120 BPM = two beats per second.

In Indian music the speed is called **laya**:

| Laya | Meaning | Rough BPM (Uncertain: ranges vary by context) |
|---|---|---|
| Vilambit | slow | under ~60 (very slow classical can be 20-40) |
| Madhya | medium | ~60-150 |
| Drut | fast | above ~150 |

### Durations

A note can last for different numbers of beats. In this curriculum's notation (from [Conventions](../05-CONVENTIONS-AND-NOTATION-KEY.md#43-rhythm-marks)), spaces separate beats:

| Write | Duration | Count it as |
|---|---|---|
| `S - ` | 2 beats (S held through the next beat) | "1 2" |
| `S` | 1 beat | "1" |
| `SR` | 2 notes in 1 beat (1/2 beat each) | "1 and" |
| `SRGm` | 4 notes in 1 beat (1/4 beat each) | "1 e and a" |
| `_` | 1 beat of silence | "(1)" |

## 4. Step-by-step instructions

### Step 1: Clap the pulse (RHY-01)

1. Metronome at 60 BPM. Listen for 4 clicks.
2. Clap exactly with each click for 1 minute. When you are exactly together, **the click "disappears" under your clap**. If you hear two sounds (clap-click or click-clap), you are early or late.
3. Repeat at 90 BPM.

### Step 2: Count aloud

Count "1 2 3 4, 1 2 3 4" with the clicks while clapping. Then **say** the counts but clap only on "1". Then clap only on "1" and "3".

### Step 3: Durations (RHY-03)

At 60 BPM, clap and say these lines. One clap per note; a held note gets one clap and you keep counting through the hold.

```
Line A:  S   S   S   S   | S   S   S   S   ||   (1 beat each)
Line B:  S   -   S   -   | S   -   S   -   ||   (2 beats each)
Line C:  SS  SS  SS  SS  | SS  SS  SS  SS  ||   (2 per beat)
Line D:  S   S   SS  S   | S   -   SS  S   ||   (mixed)
Line E:  SSSS S  SS  S   | SS  SSSS S   -  ||   (includes 4 per beat)
Line F:  S   _   S   _   | SS  _   S   -   ||   (with rests)
```

For 2 per beat, say "1-and, 2-and". For 4 per beat, say "1-e-and-a" (four even syllables, as in "ta-ka-di-mi", a common Indian rhythm syllable pattern).

### Step 4: Apply to the flute (after L04)

Play line A on Sa at 60 BPM, then line B. Each note starts exactly on the click. In line B, hold the note through the second click, not stopping early.

### Step 5: Steady counting (RHY-04)

1. Play or clap line A at 60 BPM for 4 bars.
2. Mute the metronome (many apps have a "gap" or "mute bars" feature; otherwise turn the volume down) for 4 bars while you continue.
3. Unmute. Are you still with the click? If you are ahead, you rushed; if behind, you dragged.

This is the RHY-04 test. Expect to fail at first; it improves over weeks.

### What you should hear

When clapping in time, you hear **one** sound per beat, not two. When playing, each note's start lines up with the click.

## 7. Guided practice (5 min daily)

1. Clap the pulse: 1 minute at 60 BPM, 1 minute at 90 BPM.
2. One of lines A-F (rotate daily), clapping and counting aloud.
3. From Day 5 (once Sa is stable): play lines A, B and C on Sa at 60 BPM.
4. From Week 2: the muted-bar test, twice.

Full drill set: [EX-RHY](../exercises/EX-RHY-rhythm-drills.md).

## 8. Independent exercises

1. **Guess the BPM:** clap a tempo you think is 60, then check with the metronome. Repeat for 90 and 120. Target: within +/-5 BPM (RHY-02).
2. Write your own 8-beat rhythm line using `S`, `-`, `SS`, `_`, then clap it.
3. Tap the pulse of 3 different songs and write down their BPM.

## 9. Common mistakes and corrections

| Mistake | How you notice | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Rushing (speeding up) | Ahead of the click after muted bars | Count aloud; relax; listen to the click, not your fingers |
| Cutting held notes short | A gap before the next note in line B | Keep the air going until the next click |
| Uneven 2-per-beat ("long-short") | Recording sounds galloping | Say "1-and" evenly; slow down |
| Clapping without listening | Two sounds per beat | Start quieter so you can hear the click |

More: [TS-09](../troubleshooting/TS-09-rhythm-and-tempo.md).

## 10. Quiz

1. What does BPM mean?
2. At 120 BPM, how many beats happen in one second?
3. How long is `S -`? How long is each note in `SRGm`?
4. What are the three laya names, from slowest to fastest?
5. Count the beats in this line: `S R G - | P _ GR S ||`

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answer key</summary>

1. Beats per minute.
2. Two.
3. `S -` is 2 beats. In `SRGm`, each note is 1/4 beat.
4. Vilambit, madhya, drut.
5. 8 beats: S, R, G, -, P, _, GR (1 beat containing two notes), S.

</details>

## 11. Mastery criteria

- [ ] Clap and play lines A-F at 60 BPM without errors (RHY-03).
- [ ] (Phase 2) After 4 muted bars, still aligned with the click (RHY-04).
