## 1. Learning objectives

- Play the four **komal** notes `r g d n` in tune with half-holes (FNG-08).
- Play the **chromatic scale** (all 12 notes) slowly and evenly (THY-04).
- Build and verify your **own komal chart** for your flute (PIT-08).
- Test **alternative (cross) fingerings** and keep only those that are verified (FNG-15).
- Recognise komal notes by ear (EX-EAR Level 6, EAR-05).

## 2. Prerequisites

- Reliable shuddha Ma half-hole, 8/10 placement hit rate ([L16](L16-shuddha-ma-and-half-holing.md)).
- Pitch control and drone listening ([L17](L17-intonation-and-drone.md)).

## 3. Simple explanation

**Komal** means "soft": a note lowered by a semitone. Four swaras can be komal: Re, Ga, Dha, Ni.
Together with tivra Ma and the 7 shuddha notes, they make **12 notes**:

```
S   r   R   g   G   m   M   P   d   D   n   N   S'
C   C#  D   D#  E   F   F#  G   G#  A   A#  B   C      (Western, Sa = C)
```

Every adjacent pair is one semitone apart.

On the bansuri, each komal note is made by **half-covering the hole** that would lower the note
from the shuddha note above it to the shuddha note below it:

| Komal | Between | Half-hole | Fingering | Register |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `.d` | `.P` and `.D` | hole 6 | `XXX XXH` | 1 |
| `.n` | `.D` and `.N` | hole 5 | `XXX XHO` | 1 |
| `r` | `S` and `R` | hole 3 | `XXH OOO` | 1 |
| `g` | `R` and `G` | hole 2 | `XHO OOO` | 1 |
| `d` | `P` and `D` | hole 6 | `XXX XXH` | 2 |
| `n` | `D` and `N` | hole 5 | `XXX XHO` | 2 |
| `r'`, `g'` | taar | holes 3, 2 | as `r`, `g` | 2 |

**Fact:** half-hole notes are the least stable on the bansuri: their tone is often weaker and
their pitch more sensitive. **Uncertain (your flute):** the exact coverage differs for each hole
and each flute. Your tuner and drone decide.

**Musical importance:** komal notes give ragas like **Bhairav** (`r`, `d`), **Kafi** (`g`, `n`),
**Bhairavi** (`r g d n`) and many film songs their emotional colour.

## 4. Step-by-step instructions

### 4.1 One komal note at a time (slide method)

For each komal note, start from the shuddha note **above** it (where the hole is open) and
slowly slide the finger **onto** the hole until the tuner shows the komal pitch. Example for `g`:

1. Play `G` (`XOO OOO`). Hole 2 is open.
2. Slide finger 2 onto about half of hole 2. The pitch falls from E toward D#.
3. Stop at D# (tuner). Hold 4 s.
3. Memorise the finger position.
4. Repeat 10 times; then practise **placing** it directly.

Order of learning (Recommendation): `n` (register 2) and `g` first (Kafi, Khamaj, many songs),
then `d`, then `r` (often hardest to keep stable), then mandra `.d .n`.

### 4.2 Tuner targets

Start with +/-20 cents; aim for +/-10 cents by the end of Phase 4. Remember the drone lock is
the final judge ([L17](L17-intonation-and-drone.md)).

### 4.3 Komal and shuddha contrast

Play pairs slowly with the drone: `g G g G`, `n N n N`, `d D d D`, `r R r R`. Feel the
emotional difference: komal notes sound "darker" or "sadder" against Sa.

### 4.4 The chromatic scale

`S r R g G m M P d D n N S'` and back down, 1 note per beat at 50 BPM. Every note must be
the **same distance** apart (one semitone). Record and check with the tuner afterwards.

### 4.5 Alternative fingerings (FNG-15)

Some players use **cross-fingerings** (a hole open above a closed one) instead of half-holes
on certain notes, because they can be more stable on particular flutes.

**Uncertain:** whether any cross-fingering is in tune on **your** flute must be tested.
Candidates worth testing (record the tuner reading in your chart):

| Note | Candidate | Test result (your flute) |
|---|---|---|
| `m` | `OXX OOO` | |
| `n` | `XXX OXX` | |
| `m` | `OXX XOO` | |

Keep a cross-fingering only if it is within +/-15 cents **and** has a good tone **and** fits
the passage. Ask a teacher to check ([PRA-05](../knowledge-tree/D19-PRA-practice.md)).

**What you should hear:** against a C drone, `g` and `n` sound dark and tender; `r` sounds
tense and serious (as in morning ragas like Bhairav). If a komal note sounds "sour" rather
than "dark", it is out of tune.

## 7. Guided practice (12-15 minutes per day)

| Minutes | Exercise |
|---|---|
| 4 | Slide-and-hold for this week's komal note, 10 reps, tuner |
| 3 | Placement hit rate: place it directly from neighbouring notes, 10 tries, log the score |
| 2 | Contrast pairs (4.3) with drone |
| 3 | Scale using it: week of `n`: Khamaj thaat `S R G m P D n S'`; week of `g`: Kafi `S R g m P D n S'`; week of `d`: Bhairav `S r G m P d N S'` (slowly) |
| 2 | Chromatic scale fragment (5 notes), then full scale from week 4 |

2. Complete your verified chart (section 5).
3. Ear training: [EX-EAR Level 6](../exercises/EX-EAR-ear-training-system.md) (komal/tivra recognition).
4. Find one film song or bhajan that uses komal Ga. Learn its first phrase with [L18](L18-song-learning-system.md).

## 9. Common mistakes and corrections

| Mistake | Correction | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| Komal notes too high (not covering enough) | Cover a little more; check tuner | [TS-05](../troubleshooting/TS-05-pitch-out-of-tune.md) |
| Weak, airy komal tone | Steadier air, flat finger pad on the hole edge | [TS-01](../troubleshooting/TS-01-no-sound-or-airy-tone.md) |
| Different every time | Placement drill daily; same finger position each time | [TS-06](../troubleshooting/TS-06-finger-leaks-and-transitions.md) |
| Hand tension from "holding" half-holes | Light touch; rest every 5 min | [TS-08](../troubleshooting/TS-08-body-pain-and-tension.md) |
| Fast passages with komal notes smudged | Slow ladder; or test a verified cross-fingering | [L26](L26-keys-transposition-and-limits.md) (instrument limits) |
