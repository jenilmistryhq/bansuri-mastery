# L16 - Shuddha Ma and the Art of Half-Holing

**Objectives:** FNG-07, PIT-08, THY-04 (introduction) | **Time span:** 2-4 weeks (half-holing keeps improving for months) | **Prerequisites:** [L14](L14-tivra-ma-and-madhya-pa-dha-ni.md), [L07](L07-long-tones-tuner-and-drone.md)

[Conventions](../05-CONVENTIONS-AND-NOTATION-KEY.md) | [Knowledge tree D05](../knowledge-tree/D05-FNG-fingering.md) | [D06](../knowledge-tree/D06-PIT-pitch.md)

## 1. Learning objectives

- Understand **shuddha** (natural) vs **vikrit** (altered: komal or tivra) notes (THY-04).
- Play **shuddha Ma** (`m`) by half-covering hole 1 (FNG-07).
- Adjust the amount of coverage by ear and tuner to put `m` in tune (PIT-08).
- Switch between `m` and `M` deliberately.
- Play the **Bilawal** scale (the Western major scale) and two reward melodies.

## 2. Prerequisites

- Clean Ga (`XOO OOO`) and tivra Ma (`OOO OOO`) ([L14](L14-tivra-ma-and-madhya-pa-dha-ni.md)).
- You can read a tuner in cents ([L07](L07-long-tones-tuner-and-drone.md)).

## 3. Simple explanation

Each of the seven swaras has a "natural" position called **shuddha**. Five of them can also be
moved by a **semitone** (the smallest step on your flute):

- Re, Ga, Dha, Ni can be lowered: **komal** (`r g d n`).
- Ma can be raised: **tivra** (`M`).
- Sa and Pa never change.

That gives **12 notes** per octave (THY-04). Your flute's simple fingerings give the notes of
Kalyan thaat (`S R G M P D N`). Every other note needs a **half-hole**: a hole partly covered.

**Shuddha Ma** sits exactly between Ga (`XOO OOO`) and tivra Ma (`OOO OOO`). So you cover
**about half** of hole 1.

**Fact:** this is the standard fingering, confirmed in written sources
([R-W02](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#2-written-lessons-and-fingering-charts), [R-W03](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#2-written-lessons-and-fingering-charts)).
**Uncertain (your flute):** "half" is approximate. Some flutes need 40%, some 60%. The tuner
decides, not the eye.
**Alternative:** some players use cross-fingerings such as `OXX OOO` for `m`. Test on your flute
with a tuner before using one ([FNG-15](../knowledge-tree/D05-FNG-fingering.md), [L25](L25-komal-swaras-and-12-notes.md)).

## 4. Step-by-step instructions

### 4.1 The slide technique (Recommendation)

Rolling or sliding the finger gives finer control than lifting it.

1. Play Ga (`XOO OOO`): only finger 1 is down.
2. **Slide** finger 1 sideways or back toward your palm (the direction that feels natural for
   your hand) so it uncovers part of the hole. Do not lift it.
3. Listen: the pitch rises smoothly as you uncover more. This rising sound is your first
   **meend** (glide), which you will study in [L23](L23-meend.md).
4. Stop when the tuner reads **F** (between E and F#).
5. Memorise the feeling: where on the finger the edge of the hole is.

### 4.2 Tuner targets

| Note | Tuner should read | Target window |
|---|---|---|
| `G` | E | +/-10 cents |
| `m` | **F** | +/-20 cents to start, +/-10 cents by M3 review |
| `M` | F# | +/-10 cents |

### 4.3 Without the slide

Once the slide is reliable, practise **placing** the half-hole directly from Ga, Pa or Re.
In real melodies there is no time to slide and search.

### 4.4 Shuddha Ma in the upper part of the scale

The note after `m` is madhya Pa (`XXX XXX`, register 2). `m -> P` means: from one half-covered
hole, close all six and change register. Practise the pair alone.

  the finger is **slid**, not lifted.
- [R-V14](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#32-single-technique-demonstrations) Manuraj Singh "half notes (Komal swar) and chromatic scale": watch the finger motion only for now; komal notes come in [L25](L25-komal-swaras-and-12-notes.md).
- [R-W02](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#2-written-lessons-and-fingering-charts) bansuriflute.co.uk advanced page, half-holing section.
- Not assessed: videos not watched for this curriculum. **Ignore** any video that says
  all-open is shuddha Ma (see [Known disagreements](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#known-disagreements-between-sources)).

**What you should hear:** with a C drone, shuddha Ma sounds **calm and settled**; tivra Ma
sounds **bright and restless**, wanting to move up to Pa. If your `m` sounds "between" and
sour, it is out of tune.

## 7. Guided practice (12-15 minutes per day)

| Minutes | Exercise | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | Slide search | `G` slide up to `m`, hold 4 s, check tuner, slide back. x 6 |
| 3 | Placement | From `G`, place `m` directly (no slide), check tuner. x 10. Count hits within +/-20 cents. |
| 2 | Ma contrast | `G m G M G m G` slowly with drone; feel and hear the difference |
| 3 | Pairs | `m P m P`, `R G m G R`, `G m P m G` at 50 BPM |
| 3 | Bilawal scale | `S R G m P D N S'` / `S' N D P m G R S` at 50 -> 60 BPM |

Log your hit rate from the placement drill each day. Aim for 8/10, then 10/10.

## 8. Independent exercises: reward melodies

**Twinkle Twinkle Little Star** (Keherwa, 8 beats per line):

```
 X                 0
 S   S   P   P  |  D   D   P   -   ||
 m   m   G   G  |  R   R   S   -   ||
 P   P   m   m  |  G   G   R   -   ||
 P   P   m   m  |  G   G   R   -   ||
 S   S   P   P  |  D   D   P   -   ||
 m   m   G   G  |  R   R   S   -   ||
```

Note: `S` to `P` is a leap from register 1 to register 2.

**Ode to Joy** (Beethoven, public domain), first phrase:

```
 G   G   m   P  |  P   m   G   R   ||
 S   S   R   G  |  G   R   R   -   ||
 G   G   m   P  |  P   m   G   R   ||
 S   S   R   G  |  R   S   S   -   ||
```

(Rhythm simplified: in the original, the first note of each line ending is slightly longer
and the next slightly shorter. Listen to a recording and copy that "long-short" feel once
the notes are secure.)

More in [EX-MEL](../exercises/EX-MEL-practice-melodies.md).

## 9. Common mistakes and corrections

| Mistake | What you hear / see | Correction | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| Looking at the hole instead of listening | `m` different every time | Close your eyes; use tuner then drone | [TS-05](../troubleshooting/TS-05-pitch-out-of-tune.md) |
| Lifting instead of sliding | Jumps to M or stays at G | Keep the finger touching the bamboo | [TS-06](../troubleshooting/TS-06-finger-leaks-and-transitions.md) |
| Pressing hard to "hold" the half-hole | Tense hand, wobbling pitch | Light touch; the bamboo edge is your guide | [TS-08](../troubleshooting/TS-08-body-pain-and-tension.md) |
| Compensating with the lips | Tone goes airy | Fix pitch with the finger first, lips second | [TS-05](../troubleshooting/TS-05-pitch-out-of-tune.md) |
| Playing M where m is written | Melody sounds "wrong" in Twinkle | Re-read notation: lowercase `m` = shuddha | [Conventions](../05-CONVENTIONS-AND-NOTATION-KEY.md) |

## 10. Quiz

1. Which swaras never change?
2. Which swara can be made tivra?
3. What is the fingering for shuddha Ma on a 6-hole bansuri?
4. In Bilawal, between which pairs of notes are the semitone steps?
5. Your `m` reads -35 cents on the tuner. What do you do with your finger?

<details markdown="1"><summary>Answer key</summary>

1. Sa and Pa.
2. Ma.
3. `HOO OOO`: hole 1 half covered, all others open.
4. G-m and N-S'.
5. Uncover a little more of hole 1 (slide the finger further off) to raise the pitch.

</details>
