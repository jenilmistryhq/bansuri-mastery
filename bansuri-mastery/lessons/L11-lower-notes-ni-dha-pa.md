# L11 - Lower Notes: Ni, Dha, Pa (Mandra Saptak)

**Objectives:** FNG-03, TON-06, BOD-04
**Time span:** Days 15-30 (the lowest note often takes 1-3 weeks to become reliable)
**Prerequisites:** [L09](L09-fingering-sa-re-ga.md), [L02](L02-posture-and-holding.md)
**Previous:** [L10](L10-reading-and-writing-sargam.md) | **Next:** [L12 - Alankars](L12-alankars.md)

---

## 1. Learning objectives

- Play mandra Ni, Dha and Pa (`.N .D .P`) cleanly (FNG-03).
- Use slow, warm, wide air for the low register (TON-06).
- Seal all six holes perfectly with the finger pads (BOD-04).

## 2. Prerequisites

Clean S R G with clean transitions ([L09](L09-fingering-sa-re-ga.md)); pad placement ([L02](L02-posture-and-holding.md)).

## 3. Simple explanation

Below Sa, you close the bottom-hand holes one at a time:

| Note | Fingering | Western (Sa = C) |
|---|---|---|
| Mandra Ni `.N` | `XXX XOO` | B4 |
| Mandra Dha `.D` | `XXX XXO` | A4 |
| Mandra Pa `.P` | `XXX XXX` | G4 (your lowest note) |

These notes are very important. Most songs and many ragas dip below Sa, and the deep, breathy mandra notes are a signature sound of the bansuri.

They're also harder than S R G, for two reasons:

1. **Sealing:** every closed hole must be completely sealed. With all six closed, a leak anywhere (even a slightly off-centre finger) makes `.P` squeak, jump up an octave, or not sound at all (Fact: low notes are the most leak-sensitive).
2. **Air:** low notes need **slower, warmer, softer** air and a slightly **larger** lip opening than middle notes. Blowing as you do for Sa often makes `.P` jump to the upper octave (a squeal or a high Pa).

## 4. Step-by-step instructions

### Step 1: The "warm hands" air

Breathe on your palm as if warming cold hands: "haaa". That's slow, warm, wide air. Compare it with the narrower "pooh" for Sa. Low notes need something between the two, closer to "haaa".

### Step 2: Mandra Ni (one step down)

1. Play Sa (`XXX OOO`).
2. Close hole 4 with the bottom index finger: `XXX XOO`. That's `.N`.
3. As you go down, **slow the air slightly** and let the lip opening relax a little. Some players also roll the flute very slightly inward (Uncertain: depends on the player).
4. Hold `.N` for 4 s. Tuner: B.
5. Practise `S .N S .N S`, slurred, 5 times.

### Step 3: Mandra Dha

1. From `.N`, close hole 5 with the bottom middle finger: `XXX XXO` = `.D`. Tuner: A.
2. `S .N .D .N S`, slurred, 5 times.

### Step 4: Mandra Pa (the challenge)

1. From `.D`, close hole 6 with the bottom ring finger: `XXX XXX` = `.P`. Tuner: G.
2. If it squeaks or jumps up:
    - **Check the seal** (the most common cause). Hold all holes closed for 10 s without blowing, then look at the ring marks on all six pads. Re-centre any partial ones.
    - **Use less air**, slower and warmer.
    - Make the lip opening a little bigger and softer.
3. **Diagnostic trick:** play `.P` and lift one finger at a time (`XXX XXO`, then `XXX XOX`, ...). If lifting a particular finger makes almost no difference, that hole was probably leaking.
4. Hold `.P` for 4 s, then 6 s.

### Step 5: Connect everything

At 60 BPM, slurred:

```
S  .N .D .P | .D .N S  -  ||
.P .D .N S  | R  G  R  S  ||
G  R  S  .N | .D .P .D .N | S - - - ||
```

### Step 6: Melodies with lower notes

**Happy Birthday (first two lines only).** The rhythm here is approximate; learn the exact rhythm by singing it.

```
.P .P .D .P S .N   (Hap-py birth-day to you)
.P .P .D .P R S    (Hap-py birth-day to you)
```

The third line needs madhya Pa and the fourth needs shuddha Ma. Those come in [L14](L14-tivra-ma-and-madhya-pa-dha-ni.md) and [L16](L16-shuddha-ma-and-half-holing.md). The full version is in [EX-MEL](../exercises/EX-MEL-practice-melodies.md).

**Original practice melody "Evening Lamp" (S R G and mandra, 16 beats):**

```
S  -  .N .D | .P -  .D .N | S  R  G  R  | S  -  -  -  ||
G  R  S  .N | .D .N S  R  | .N .D .P .D | .P -  -  -  ||
```

### What you should hear

- `.N .D .P` sound **deep, soft and breathy-warm**: lower and "bigger" than Sa, not louder.
- Each note steps down cleanly.
- `.P` is a steady low tone, not a squeal and not a high Pa. If the tuner shows G but the note sounds bright and high, you're in the wrong octave (G5 instead of G4). Check the octave number.

## 5. Fingering chart

```
            hole:  1   2   3     4   5   6     Western
 Sa   S           [X] [X] [X]   [O] [O] [O]    C5
 Ni   .N          [X] [X] [X]   [X] [O] [O]    B4
 Dha  .D          [X] [X] [X]   [X] [X] [O]    A4
 Pa   .P          [X] [X] [X]   [X] [X] [X]    G4  (lowest)

 Air:   S  ("pooh") -----------> .P ("haaa")
        narrower, a bit faster    wider, slower, warmer
```

## 7. Guided practice (blocks B and C, about 15 min daily)

1. Long tones (block B): `.N`, `.D`, `.P`, 2 x 6 s each, with the tuner. Rest between them.
2. Seal check: 10 s silent hold of `XXX XXX`, then look at the pads. Once a day.
3. Step-down drill: `S .N`, `.N .D`, `.D .P` pairs, slurred, 5 reps each.
4. Lines in Step 5: 3 clean repetitions each at 60 BPM.
5. Melodies: Happy Birthday lines 1-2 and "Evening Lamp", 3 clean times each.

## 8. Independent exercises

1. Write and play a 16-beat melody using `.P .D .N S R G` that starts and ends on S.
2. Play `.P` softly, then medium. Does it stay in the low octave? Find the loudest volume at which it doesn't jump.
3. Record `.P .D .N S R G R S .N .D .P` slowly and score it with the rubric.

## 9. Common mistakes and corrections

| Mistake | How you notice | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| `.P` jumps up an octave | High, bright note; tuner shows G5 | Less air, slower and warmer; bigger lip opening |
| `.P` squeaks or gurgles | Unstable, mixed sound | Seal check; re-centre the pads; lighter but complete pressure |
| A bottom finger slightly off its hole | Partial ring mark on the pad | Move the hand slightly; the pad, not the tip, covers the hole |
| Tensing to "press harder" | Sore hands, wobbly flute | Lightest complete seal; check the thumb support |
| Low notes too loud | Breaking tone | Low notes are naturally soft; accept it |
| Neglecting the change back up to S | Blip from `.P` to `S` (three fingers move!) | Practise `.P S .P S` slowly, glued fingers |

More: [TS-03](../troubleshooting/TS-03-low-notes-wont-sound.md), [TS-06](../troubleshooting/TS-06-finger-leaks-and-transitions.md), [TS-02](../troubleshooting/TS-02-squeaks-and-wrong-octave.md).

## 10. Performance test

1. `.P` held for 6 s, clean, **5 times out of 5** (each time from a fresh start).
2. Play at 60 BPM, recorded: `S .N .D .P .D .N S - ||` three times in a row.

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Pass standard</summary>

- Part 1: 5/5 attempts sound in the low octave (tuner G4) with no squeak.
- Part 2: correct notes, no blips, in time. Rubric: T3 P2 R3 Tr3 or better.

</details>

## 11. Quiz

1. Write the fingerings for `.N`, `.D` and `.P`.
2. How should your air change as you go below Sa?
3. `.P` squeaks or will not sound. What is the most common cause, and how do you check it?
4. Your tuner shows G, but the note sounds bright and high. What has happened?
5. Why does the change from `.P` back up to `S` often produce a blip?

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answer key</summary>

1. `.N` = `XXX XOO`, `.D` = `XXX XXO`, `.P` = `XXX XXX`.
2. Slower, warmer and softer ("haaa", like warming cold hands), with a slightly larger, relaxed lip opening.
3. A leak in one of the six holes. Hold all holes closed for 10 s without blowing, then look for full ring marks on every finger pad; re-centre any partial ones.
4. You are in the wrong octave (G5 instead of G4). Use less, slower, warmer air.
5. Three fingers (holes 4, 5 and 6) must lift at the same instant. Practise `.P S .P S` slowly with "glued" fingers.

</details>

## 12. Mastery criteria
