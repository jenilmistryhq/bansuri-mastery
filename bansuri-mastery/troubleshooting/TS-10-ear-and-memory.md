# TS-10: Ear and Memory Problems

[Troubleshooting home](README.md) | Related: [EAR-01](../knowledge-tree/D10-EAR-ear.md) to EAR-10, [PIT-05](../knowledge-tree/D06-PIT-pitch.md), [PRF-04](../knowledge-tree/D18-PRF-performance.md), [SNG-08](../knowledge-tree/D13-SNG-songs.md) | Lessons: [L05](../lessons/L05-what-is-music-pitch-sa-sargam.md), [L18](../lessons/L18-song-learning-system.md), [L35](../lessons/L35-performance-and-interpretation.md)

## Symptoms

- "All notes sound the same to me"; you cannot tell whether a note went up or down.
- You cannot sing back a note you just heard.
- You hear a phrase, but by the time you pick up the flute it is gone.
- When transcribing, you guess and get most notes wrong.
- Pieces learnt last month are forgotten.
- You can play a piece only from the beginning; if you stop in the middle, you are lost.

**Important:** almost everyone can develop a good ear. People who say "I'm tone deaf" are
usually untrained, not unable. True tone deafness (amusia) is uncommon. Progress may feel slow
at first and then speed up.

## Quick checks

1. **Higher/lower test:** play two notes on the flute (or ask someone to) and say which is
   higher. Score out of 10.
2. **Sing-back test:** play Sa with the drone, then sing it. Check with a tuner app on your
   voice. Within about 50 cents?
3. **Phrase length test:** how many notes can you hear and play back correctly? 2? 4? 6?
   This tells you the right phrase length to practise at.
4. **Memory test:** pick a piece learnt 3 weeks ago; can you play it without notation?

## Causes, most likely first

| # | Cause | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Not singing** | The voice connects hearing and knowing. Indian music has always been taught by singing. |
| 2 | **No reference pitch** | Without a drone, notes have no "home" to be heard against. |
| 3 | **Phrases too long** | Memory holds a few notes at a time; longer phrases must be chunked. |
| 4 | **Guessing instead of checking** | Guesses that are never checked do not train the ear. |
| 5 | **No spaced review** | Pieces not revisited are forgotten (normal memory behaviour). |
| 6 | **Learning only from notation** | Pieces stored as "reading" rather than as sound and pattern are fragile. |

## Fixes and drills

### Fix A: Sing everything (5 minutes daily)

With the drone on C: sing `S`, then `S R G R S`, then the alankar you are practising, **before**
playing it. If singing on C5 is too high, sing an octave lower; that is fine
([PIT-05](../knowledge-tree/D06-PIT-pitch.md)). A tuner app on your voice helps at first.

### Fix B: Higher/lower and pitch matching (5 minutes)

Follow levels 1-2 of [EX-EAR](../exercises/EX-EAR-ear-training-system.md). Stay at a level until
you score 18/20, then move on. Use [R-E02](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#73-ear-training) (Functional Ear Trainer) if you
want an app.

### Fix C: Chunking (for songs and transcription)

Cut the melody into pieces of **2-4 notes**. Loop the piece ([R-A14, R-A17](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#72-tuner-metronome-recording-slow-down)),
sing it 3 times, then find it on the flute. Join chunks: 1, 2, 1+2, 3, 1+2+3...

### Fix D: Verify, never guess

When unsure between two notes, **play both** along with the recording and choose the one that
matches. Compare by singing. Write a `?` above any note you are still unsure of and return to
it later ([L18](../lessons/L18-song-learning-system.md)).

### Fix E: Self-recorded dictation (10 minutes, 3 times a week)

Play random note sequences from the lists in [EX-EAR](../exercises/EX-EAR-ear-training-system.md),
recording yourself. Two or more days later, transcribe the recording and check against the list.

### Fix F: Memory with multiple routes (Phase 3+)

For each piece:

- **Sing** it without the flute.
- **Write** it from memory.
- **Start from 4 marked points** (beginning, middle of sthayi, antara, last line).
- **Play it at half tempo** in your head while away from the flute.
Then review on a spaced schedule: next day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks, monthly
([PRA-03](../knowledge-tree/D19-PRA-practice.md)).

## How to verify the fix

- Ear-training scores rising week by week in the tracker.
- Transcription of an 8-note melody exact (notes and rhythm) 4 times out of 5 ([EAR-08](../knowledge-tree/D10-EAR-ear.md)).
- Any 3 random repertoire pieces playable on demand ([SNG-08](../knowledge-tree/D13-SNG-songs.md)).

## When to seek a teacher

- After 6 weeks of daily singing practice you still cannot tell higher from lower reliably.
  A teacher can test you properly; in rare cases, a hearing check is sensible.
- Call-and-response with a live teacher (guru-shishya style) is the fastest ear trainer
  there is. Even occasional lessons help a lot.
