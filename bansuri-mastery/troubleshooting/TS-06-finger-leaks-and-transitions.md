# TS-06: Finger Leaks, Blips Between Notes, Uneven Fast Passages

[Troubleshooting home](README.md) | Related: [BOD-04](../knowledge-tree/D02-BOD-body.md), [FNG-06](../knowledge-tree/D05-FNG-fingering.md), FNG-07, FNG-13, FNG-14 | Lessons: [L02](../lessons/L02-posture-and-holding.md), [L09](../lessons/L09-fingering-sa-re-ga.md), [L12](../lessons/L12-alankars.md), [L16](../lessons/L16-shuddha-ma-and-half-holing.md)

## Symptoms

- **Blips:** a short extra note sounds between two notes (for example going `R` to `.N` you
  hear a quick `S` or `.D` in between).
- **Leaks:** a note sounds airy, wobbly or wrong, especially low notes.
- **Wrong note:** you finger Dha and hear Ni.
- **Uneven speed:** in alankars some notes rush and others drag.
- **Half-hole notes land in a different place every time.**

## Quick checks

1. **Slow motion test.** Play the troublesome change (for example `R` to `.N`) very slowly,
   watching your fingers in a mirror or on a phone video. Do all moving fingers move at the
   same moment?
2. **Height test.** How high do your fingers lift? More than about 1-2 cm above the holes
   makes them slow and inaccurate.
3. **Mark test.** After playing, look at your finger pads: are the hole marks complete
   circles (see TS-03, Fix A)?
4. **Recording test.** Record the passage and play it back at half speed (YouTube or
   Audacity, [R-A13](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#72-tuner-metronome-recording-slow-down)). Blips are much easier to hear slowed down.

## Causes, most likely first

| # | Cause | Typical example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fingers not moving together** | `S` to `.D` needs holes 4 and 5 to close at once. One arrives earlier: blip. |
| 2 | **Fingers lifting too high** | Slow return, blips, uneven rhythm |
| 3 | **Fingertips instead of pads** | Leaks, especially on holes 4-6 |
| 4 | **Too much grip/pressure** | Slows fingers, causes tension and shaking |
| 5 | **Playing faster than your control** | Everything gets uneven; practise slower |
| 6 | **Half-hole position not memorised** | `m` lands too sharp or too flat |
| 7 | **"Cross" changes** (one finger up while another goes down, for example `G` to `m` or `.N` to `R`) | The hardest type of finger change |

## Fixes and drills

### Fix A: Pair drill for blips (5 minutes)

1. Pick the problem pair, for example `R` and `.N`.
2. At 50 BPM, play `R - .N - R - .N -` with slurs, watching your fingers.
3. Think "**snap**": all fingers move in one decisive movement.
4. 20 clean repetitions. If any repetition has a blip, the count restarts at 0 (strict, but
   it works).
5. Raise the metronome 4 BPM only after 20 clean repetitions.

Common difficult pairs: `S`-`.D`, `R`-`.N`, `G`-`.N`, `S`-`.P`, `G`-`m`, `M`-`P`, `N`-`S'`.

### Fix B: Low fingers (3 minutes)

Play `S R G R S` and keep the lifted fingers **hovering just above the holes** (about the
thickness of a coin to 1 cm). Use a mirror. Slow is fine.

### Fix C: Minimum pressure (2 minutes)

Hold `.P`. Slowly reduce finger pressure until the note starts to fail. Then add just enough
pressure to make it clean. Remember that feeling: this is all the pressure you need.

### Fix D: Metronome ladder for fast passages (10 minutes)

From [L01](../lessons/L01-how-to-practise.md):

1. Find the tempo where you can play the passage **perfectly 3 times in a row**.
2. Raise by 4 BPM. Repeat 3 perfect times.
3. When you fail twice at a tempo, go back 8 BPM and finish the day there.
4. Write the highest clean tempo in your log. Next day, start 8 BPM below it.

### Fix E: Rhythm variations for evenness (5 minutes, Phase 3+)

Play an alankar with 2 notes per beat in a **long-short** pattern, then **short-long**, then
even. This trains each finger movement separately and evens out the passage.

### Fix F: Half-hole memory (5 minutes)

See [TS-05](TS-05-pitch-out-of-tune.md) Fix D and [L16](../lessons/L16-shuddha-ma-and-half-holing.md).
Roll or slide the finger off the hole; do not lift it straight up. Practise `G m G m` 20
times, checking with the tuner every 5.

## How to verify the fix

- Slowed-down recording shows **no blips** in the pair or passage.
- The passage is clean 3/3 at the target tempo on two separate days.
- Low notes clean 5/5 (shows the seal is fixed).

## When to seek a teacher

- Your hand size makes a stretch impossible without strain (see [TS-08](TS-08-body-pain-and-tension.md)).
- A finger shakes or will not move independently despite relaxation work.
- You want to check whether your grip (fingertips vs piper's grip) suits your hands.
