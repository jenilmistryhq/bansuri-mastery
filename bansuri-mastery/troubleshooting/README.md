# Troubleshooting: Find the Cause Before You Fix It

[Home](../README.md) | [Conventions](../05-CONVENTIONS-AND-NOTATION-KEY.md) | Objective: [PRA-04](../knowledge-tree/D19-PRA-practice.md)

Most practice time lost by self-taught players goes into "trying harder" at the wrong
thing. A squeak can come from a leaking finger, from blowing too hard, or from a tense lip,
and each needs a different fix. These guides help you **diagnose first, then practise the
right fix**.

## 1. The diagnostic tree

Start at the top and follow the branch that describes what you notice.

```
What is wrong?
|
+-- SOUND PROBLEMS
|   +-- No sound at all, or mostly air/hiss ..................... TS-01
|   +-- Sudden squeak, or note jumps to a higher note ........... TS-02
|   +-- Low notes (.N .D .P) will not sound or crack ............ TS-03
|   +-- Upper notes (madhya P D N, taar) will not come out ...... TS-04
|
+-- PITCH PROBLEMS
|   +-- Notes are flat/sharp on the tuner or against the drone .. TS-05
|   +-- Half-hole notes (m r g d n) are unstable ................ TS-05 then TS-06
|
+-- FINGER PROBLEMS
|   +-- Extra "blip" notes between notes, or wrong note sounds .. TS-06
|   +-- Fast passages are uneven or messy ....................... TS-06
|
+-- BODY PROBLEMS
|   +-- Dizziness, out of breath, cannot hold notes ............. TS-07
|   +-- Pain, stiffness, numbness, tingling, jaw problems ....... TS-08  (read the medical caution)
|
+-- TIME AND MEMORY PROBLEMS
|   +-- Rushing, dragging, losing sam, losing the beat .......... TS-09
|   +-- Cannot hear notes, cannot remember phrases or pieces .... TS-10
|
+-- PROGRESS PROBLEMS
|   +-- No improvement for weeks, boredom, skipping practice .... TS-11
|
+-- INSTRUMENT PROBLEMS
    +-- Crack, loose binding, flute "feels different", care ..... TS-12
```

## 2. The general diagnosis method (use it for any problem)

Do these five steps in order. Write the answers in your practice log ([PRF-03](../knowledge-tree/D18-PRF-performance.md)).

### Step 1: Describe the problem exactly

Not "my playing is bad", but "Ga cracks about 1 time in 3 when I come from Sa, slurred, at
60 BPM". Record 30 seconds and listen back before you decide what is happening
([L08](../lessons/L08-recording-and-self-assessment.md)). Your ears while playing are not
reliable; the recording is.

### Step 2: Isolate it

Ask these questions one by one. Each answer cuts the list of possible causes.

| Question | If YES, the cause is probably... |
|---|---|
| Does it happen on **one note only**? | That note's fingering (a leak or the wrong finger) or a half-hole, so see TS-06/TS-05 |
| Does it happen in **one register only** (only low, only high)? | Air speed or lip opening for that register, so see TS-03/TS-04 |
| Does it happen only on **one transition** (for example `G` to `R`)? | Finger timing, so see TS-06 |
| Does it happen only **after 15+ minutes**? | Fatigue (lip, breath, hands), so see TS-07/TS-08 and take breaks |
| Does it happen only **at faster tempos**? | You are practising above your control level, so see TS-06 and TS-09 |
| Does it happen only **when nervous or recording**? | Tension, so see TS-08 and [L35](../lessons/L35-performance-and-interpretation.md) |
| Did it **start suddenly** after the flute was fine? | Check the instrument first, so see TS-12 |

### Step 3: Form one hypothesis

Pick the **single most likely cause** from the relevant TS guide. Beginners' problems are
usually simple: a leaking finger, blowing too hard, or tension. Rule these out before
assuming anything unusual.

### Step 4: Change one thing only

Test your hypothesis by changing one variable: finger pressure, OR air speed, OR flute
angle, OR tempo. If you change three things at once you will not know which one helped.

### Step 5: Verify with evidence

- **Tuner** for pitch problems ([L07](../lessons/L07-long-tones-tuner-and-drone.md)).
- **Recording** for tone, blips and rhythm ([L08](../lessons/L08-recording-and-self-assessment.md)).
- **Count** clean attempts: a fix is real only when you get **5 clean out of 5**, on two
  different days.

If the fix does not work after about a week of correct practice, go back to Step 3 with the
next most likely cause. If two hypotheses fail, ask a teacher
([PRA-05](../knowledge-tree/D19-PRA-practice.md)) and bring your recordings.

## 3. Quick reference table

| Symptom | Most likely causes for a beginner (most likely first) | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| No sound / airy sound | Air aimed wrong; lip opening too big; covering too little or too much of the blowing hole; blowing too hard | [TS-01](TS-01-no-sound-or-airy-tone.md) |
| Squeaks / note jumps up an octave | Blowing too hard or too fast; leaking finger; lip opening too small | [TS-02](TS-02-squeaks-and-wrong-octave.md) |
| Low notes do not sound | Leak in holes 4-6; air too fast; flute rolled out | [TS-03](TS-03-low-notes-wont-sound.md) |
| High notes do not sound | Air too slow or too wide; forcing instead of focusing; leak | [TS-04](TS-04-high-notes.md) |
| Out of tune | Flute angle; air speed; half-hole coverage; cold flute | [TS-05](TS-05-pitch-out-of-tune.md) |
| Blips between notes | Fingers not moving together; lifting fingers too high | [TS-06](TS-06-finger-leaks-and-transitions.md) |
| Dizziness / short breath | Over-breathing; shallow breathing; wasting air | [TS-07](TS-07-breath-dizziness-and-endurance.md) |
| Pain / numbness | Gripping too hard; raised shoulders; too-long sessions | [TS-08](TS-08-body-pain-and-tension.md) |
| Rushing / dragging | No metronome; slowing on hard notes; not counting | [TS-09](TS-09-rhythm-and-tempo.md) |
| Cannot hear or remember notes | Not singing; phrases too long; no drone reference | [TS-10](TS-10-ear-and-memory.md) |
| No progress, low motivation | Unstructured practice; tasks too hard or too easy; no review | [TS-11](TS-11-practice-plateaus-and-motivation.md) |
| Crack, damage, flute changed | Dry air; heat; dropped flute | [TS-12](TS-12-flute-damage-and-care.md) |

## 4. When to stop self-diagnosing

Get outside help (a teacher, an experienced player, or the flute maker) when:

- the same problem has resisted two different, correctly practised fixes for 2+ weeks;
- you cannot tell from recordings what is happening;
- you suspect the flute itself (TS-12);
- **any pain persists** or you feel numbness or tingling (TS-08: see a doctor or physiotherapist).

A 30-minute online lesson with your recordings ready often solves what weeks of guessing
cannot. See [R-C01 to R-C03](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#4-courses-and-teachers-paid-or-structured).
