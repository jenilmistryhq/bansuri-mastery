# TS-07: Dizziness, Running Out of Breath, Low Endurance

[Troubleshooting home](README.md) | Related: [BRE-01](../knowledge-tree/D04-BRE-breath.md), BRE-02, BRE-03, BRE-04, BRE-06, BRE-07, [BOD-07](../knowledge-tree/D02-BOD-body.md) | Lessons: [L03](../lessons/L03-breathing-basics.md), [L07](../lessons/L07-long-tones-tuner-and-drone.md)

> **Safety first.** If you feel dizzy, light-headed, see spots, or feel tingling around the
> mouth or in the fingers: **stop playing, sit down, and breathe normally and gently through
> the nose** until it passes (usually within a minute or two). Do not stand up quickly. If
> dizziness happens even when you are not playing, if you faint, or if you have chest pain,
> heart or lung conditions, or high blood pressure, see a doctor before continuing
> wind-instrument practice. This guide is not medical advice.

## Symptoms

- Light-headedness after a few minutes of long tones (very common in the first weeks).
- Gasping for air in the middle of phrases.
- Notes fading or going flat at the end of each breath.
- Tired chest or stomach muscles; tired lips after 15-20 minutes.
- Noisy inhales that interrupt the music.

## Quick checks

1. **When does dizziness happen?** During long-tone practice with many deep breaths in a row?
   That is over-breathing (cause 1), the most common cause.
2. **Shoulder test.** Breathe in deeply in front of a mirror. Do your shoulders rise? If yes,
   you are breathing high in the chest (cause 3).
3. **Air waste test.** Hold your palm in front of the blowing hole while playing Sa. A wide,
   strong rush of air past the flute means much of your breath is not making sound (cause 2).
4. **Timing test.** Hold Sa with a timer. Under 6 seconds in Week 3+ suggests air waste or
   shallow breathing.

## Causes, most likely first

| # | Cause | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Over-breathing (hyperventilation)** | Taking many deep breaths quickly lowers carbon dioxide in the blood, causing dizziness. Very common when practising long tones. |
| 2 | **Wasting air** | A big lip opening or poor aim sends air past the flute (see TS-01). |
| 3 | **Shallow chest breathing** | Small breaths, raised shoulders, tension. |
| 4 | **No breathing plan** | You play until empty instead of breathing at phrase ends. |
| 5 | **Fatigue** | Sessions without breaks; lips and breathing muscles tire. |
| 6 | **Blowing harder than needed** | Uses up the breath quickly and makes tone harsh. |

## Fixes and drills

### Fix A: Rest-breath pattern (use from Day 1)

During long-tone practice, after every note **take 2-3 normal, relaxed breaths** before the
next deep breath. One long tone, then a natural rest. Never take deep breaths back to back.
Keep long-tone blocks to 5 minutes, then do something lighter (fingering without blowing,
clapping, theory).

### Fix B: Low breathing (3 minutes daily, Phase 0-1)

Lie on your back, one hand on your belly, one on your chest. Breathe so the belly hand rises
and the chest hand stays almost still. 10 slow breaths. Then do the same sitting in playing
posture. See [L03](../lessons/L03-breathing-basics.md).

### Fix C: Hiss timer (2 minutes)

Breathe in low, then hiss "sssss" through your teeth as **evenly** as possible. Time it.
Target 20 s, then 30 s ([BRE-03](../knowledge-tree/D04-BRE-breath.md)). Rest 3 normal breaths
between attempts. 3 attempts.

### Fix D: Fix air waste (see TS-01)

A smaller, better-aimed airstream makes the same breath last much longer. Most early
"breath problems" are really tone problems.

### Fix E: Breath marks (Phase 2+)

Before playing a melody, mark breaths with `,` at phrase ends in the notation
([BRE-04](../knowledge-tree/D04-BRE-breath.md)). Breathe **before** you are empty: plan to
use about 70% of the breath per phrase.

### Fix F: Quick silent inhale (3 minutes)

At 60 BPM: play `S - - -`, breathe in during one beat through the mouth corners, play
`R - - -`, and so on. The inhale should be quiet and fast. Noisy inhales usually come from a
tight throat; keep it open as in a relaxed yawn.

### Fix G: Lip endurance

Lips tire before breath does. Rest the lips for 1-2 minutes every 15 minutes (the
practice template in [L01](../lessons/L01-how-to-practise.md) builds this in). Endurance
grows over weeks; you cannot force it.

## How to verify the fix

- A full 15-minute block with **no dizziness** using the rest-breath pattern.
- Long tones: 12 s by the end of Phase 1, 20 s by Phase 3 ([BRE-07](../knowledge-tree/D04-BRE-breath.md)).
- A 16-beat melody played with **no unplanned breaths** ([BRE-04](../knowledge-tree/D04-BRE-breath.md)).

## When to seek help

- **Doctor:** dizziness when not playing, fainting, chest pain, breathlessness out of
  proportion to effort, or any existing heart/lung condition.
- **Teacher:** you breathe correctly and do not waste air, but still cannot exceed 6-8
  seconds after a month.
