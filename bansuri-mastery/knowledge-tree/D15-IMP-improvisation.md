# D15 - IMP: Improvisation

[Back to Knowledge Tree](../01-KNOWLEDGE-TREE.md) | Symbols: [Conventions](../05-CONVENTIONS-AND-NOTATION-KEY.md)

```
IMP Improvisation
+-- First steps ........ IMP-01 call and response, IMP-02 limited-note improvising over drone
+-- Variation .......... IMP-03 varying a melody
+-- Raga-based ......... IMP-04 swar vistar within rules, IMP-05 rhythmic improvisation (taans, tihais)
+-- Song context ....... IMP-06 fills and interludes
+-- Long form .......... IMP-07 shaping a long improvisation
```

| ID | Subtopic | Understand / perform | Why it matters | Prereqs | Diff | Imp | Mastery test | Phase | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IMP-01 | Call and response | Answer a 2-bar phrase with your own 2-bar phrase using the same notes | Low-pressure first improvising; trains listening | FNG-02, EAR-09 | 2 | Core | 8 answer phrases in a row without stopping | P2 | [L29](../lessons/L29-improvisation-foundations.md) |
| IMP-02 | Limited notes | Improvise 1 minute using only 3 notes, then the pentatonic `S R G P D`, over a drone | Constraints make improvising possible | IMP-01, PIT-03 | 2 | Core | 2-min recorded improvisation that stays in time/pulse and ends on Sa | P2-P3 | [L29](../lessons/L29-improvisation-foundations.md) |
| IMP-03 | Variations | Vary a known melody: rhythm, ornament, octave, note substitutions | Bridge from repertoire to creativity | IMP-02, SNG-02 | 3 | High | 4 distinct variations of one melody, recorded | P3 | [L29](../lessons/L29-improvisation-foundations.md) |
| IMP-04 | Raga improvisation | Develop a raga gradually, obeying aroh/avroh, nyas notes and pakad | Heart of Hindustani performance | RAG-09 | 4 | Core | 5-min Yaman improvisation passing the raga checklist | P4 | [L31](../lessons/L31-alap-and-swar-vistar.md) |
| IMP-05 | Rhythmic | Improvise taans and tihais that land on sam | Excitement and structure | RHY-11, RAG-11 | 5 | High | 3 improvised tihais landing on sam with tabla loop | P4-P5 | [L32](../lessons/L32-bandish-taans-layakari-tihai.md) |
| IMP-06 | Fills | Improvise short fills and interlude phrases in songs without clashing with the vocal line | Accompaniment | ENS-03, THY-10 | 4 | Medium | Fills on a song recording judged appropriate (not overplaying) | P4 | [L34](../lessons/L34-accompaniment-and-ensemble.md) |
| IMP-07 | Long form | Shape a 10-20-min improvisation with build-up, climax and resolution | Concert-level musicianship | IMP-04, IMP-05 | 5 | High | Milestone M10 | P5 | [L37](../lessons/L37-full-raga-presentation-and-independence.md) |
