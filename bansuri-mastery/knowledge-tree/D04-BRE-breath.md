# D04 - BRE: Breath Management

[Back to Knowledge Tree](../01-KNOWLEDGE-TREE.md) | Symbols: [Conventions](../05-CONVENTIONS-AND-NOTATION-KEY.md)

```
BRE Breath
+-- Mechanics ...... BRE-01 low (abdominal) breathing, BRE-02 quick inhale, BRE-03 controlled exhale
+-- Safety ......... BRE-06 avoiding dizziness/hyperventilation
+-- Musical use .... BRE-04 phrase breathing, BRE-05 support for dynamics, BRE-07 endurance
+-- Advanced ....... BRE-08 circular breathing (optional)
```

| ID | Subtopic | Understand / perform | Why it matters | Prereqs | Diff | Imp | Mastery test | Phase | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BRE-01 | Low breathing | Breathe so the belly and lower ribs expand, shoulders stay down | Larger, calmer air supply | BOD-01 | 1 | Core | Hand-on-belly check: belly/ribs move, shoulders still, 10 breaths | P0 | [L03](../lessons/L03-breathing-basics.md) |
| BRE-02 | Quick inhale | Take a full silent breath through the mouth corners in about 1 beat at 60 BPM | Breathing must fit inside music | BRE-01 | 2 | High | 4 quick breaths in a phrase drill at 60 BPM without gasping or noise | P1 | [L03](../lessons/L03-breathing-basics.md) |
| BRE-03 | Controlled exhale | Release air steadily (hiss "sss" test) for 20 s, then 30 s | Steady air = steady tone and pitch | BRE-01 | 1 | Core | Even hiss 30 s timed | P0-P1 | [L03](../lessons/L03-breathing-basics.md) |
| BRE-04 | Phrase breathing | Plan breath marks at phrase ends; never breathe mid-word of a melody | Musical phrasing; avoids running out of air | BRE-02, NOT-01 | 2 | High | Mark breaths in a 16-beat melody and play it with no unplanned breaths | P2 | [L03](../lessons/L03-breathing-basics.md), [L18](../lessons/L18-song-learning-system.md) |
| BRE-05 | Support for dynamics | Increase/decrease air speed smoothly for crescendo/diminuendo without collapsing tone | Expression | BRE-03, TON-08 | 4 | High | 8-s swell on Ga, smooth, judged on recording | P3 | [L17](../lessons/L17-intonation-and-drone.md) |
| BRE-06 | Safety | Recognise dizziness from over-breathing; rest, breathe normally, sit down | Beginners commonly get light-headed | none | 1 | Core | Can state the dizziness protocol | P0 | [L03](../lessons/L03-breathing-basics.md) |
| BRE-07 | Endurance | Sustain a clear note for 20 s+; play 2-min pieces without fatigue | Alap and slow songs need long breaths | BRE-03, TON-04 | 3 | High | 20 s long tone on Sa and on `.P`; 2-min piece without tone collapse | P3 | [L07](../lessons/L07-long-tones-tuner-and-drone.md) |
| BRE-08 | Circular breathing (Enrich, Disputed) | Know what it is; optionally learn it. It is not required for Hindustani bansuri and many masters rarely use it | Occasionally used for very long phrases or effects | BRE-07 | 5 | Enrich | Optional: 30 s continuous tone with circular breathing | P5 | [L37](../lessons/L37-full-raga-presentation-and-independence.md) |
