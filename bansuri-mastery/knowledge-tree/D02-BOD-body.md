|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BOD-01 | Sitting posture | Sit cross-legged or on a chair with a tall, relaxed spine, open chest, head level | Breath capacity and long-term comfort | none | 1 | Core | Hold posture for a 10-min session; photo/video check against the checklist | P0 | [L02](../lessons/L02-posture-and-holding.md) |
| BOD-02 | Standing posture | Stand balanced, knees soft, flute level, for performance situations | Needed for stage and ensemble playing | BOD-01 | 2 | Medium | Play a 3-min piece standing with no posture faults on video | P3 | [L35](../lessons/L35-performance-and-interpretation.md) |
| BOD-03 | Holding | Choose and fix your side (left or right), place hands correctly, support the flute with thumbs and the lip contact point | A stable hold frees the fingers and keeps the blowing hole steady | INS-01 | 1 | Core | Hold the flute in playing position 60 s with all holes sealed, no wobble | P0-P1 | [L02](../lessons/L02-posture-and-holding.md) |
| BOD-04 | Finger pads | Cover holes with the flat pads, fingers slightly curved/flat, minimum pressure needed to seal | Leaks are the most common cause of wrong notes and squeaks | BOD-03 | 1 | Core | Mandra Pa (`XXX XXX`) sounds cleanly 5 times out of 5 | P1 | [L02](../lessons/L02-posture-and-holding.md), [L11](../lessons/L11-lower-notes-ni-dha-pa.md) |
| BOD-05 | Relaxation | Scan and release shoulders, neck, jaw, wrists and fingers while playing | Tension ruins tone, speed and causes pain | BOD-01 | 2 | Core | 2-min tension scan done at the start and middle of every session (tracker); video shows no raised shoulders | P0 onward | [L02](../lessons/L02-posture-and-holding.md) |
| BOD-06 | Warm-up | Do a 3-5 min warm-up: shoulder rolls, wrist and finger stretches, breathing | Reduces strain, prepares coordination | none | 1 | High | Warm-up done before every session for 2 weeks | P0 onward | [L01](../lessons/L01-how-to-practise.md) |
| BOD-07 | Session limits | Split practice into blocks, rest lips and hands, stop when dizzy or in pain | Prevents injury and lip fatigue; sustainable progress | BRE-06 | 1 | Core | Practice log shows breaks every 15-20 min | P0 onward | [L01](../lessons/L01-how-to-practise.md) |
| BOD-08 | Injury prevention | Recognise warning signs (persistent pain, numbness, tingling, jaw clicking, wrist pain) and know when to rest or see a doctor or physiotherapist | Repetitive strain can stop you playing for months | BOD-05 | 2 | High | Can list the warning signs and the response for each | P1 | [TS-08](../troubleshooting/TS-08-body-pain-and-tension.md) |
