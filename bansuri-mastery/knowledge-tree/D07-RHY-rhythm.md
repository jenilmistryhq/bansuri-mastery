# D07 - RHY: Rhythm, Laya and Tala

```
RHY Rhythm
+-- Pulse and tempo ......... RHY-01 pulse, RHY-02 tempo/laya, RHY-04 steady counting
+-- Durations ............... RHY-03 1, 2, 1/2, 1/4 beat notes
+-- Tala .................... RHY-05 concepts, RHY-06 keherwa/dadra, RHY-07 teentaal, RHY-08 rupak/jhaptaal/ektaal
+-- Applied ................. RHY-09 starting points (mukhda), RHY-12 Western meters and syncopation, RHY-14 playing with tabla/lehra
+-- Advanced ................ RHY-10 layakari, RHY-11 tihai, RHY-13 vilambit laya
```

| RHY-01 | Pulse | Feel and clap a steady beat with a metronome | All music sits on a pulse | none | 1 | Core | Clap with the metronome at 60 and 90 BPM for 60 s, clicks "disappear" under the claps | P0-P1 | [L06](../lessons/L06-pulse-and-tempo.md) |

| RHY-04 | Steady counting | Keep counting (aloud, then silently) while playing; do not rush or slow on hard notes | Stability is the first thing listeners notice | RHY-03 | 2 | Core | Play an alankar with the metronome muted for 4 bars; on re-entry you are still aligned | P2 | [L06](../lessons/L06-pulse-and-tempo.md) |

| RHY-07 | Teentaal | Recite and clap Teentaal (16: 4+4+4+4, sam 1, khali 9) | The most common classical tala | RHY-05 | 2 | Core | Recite with claps/waves 4 cycles; land on sam after counting silently 2 cycles | P2 | [L15](../lessons/L15-taal-basics.md) |
