# D10 - EAR: Ear Training

[Back to Knowledge Tree](../01-KNOWLEDGE-TREE.md) | Symbols: [Conventions](../05-CONVENTIONS-AND-NOTATION-KEY.md) | Exercise system: [EX-EAR](../exercises/EX-EAR-ear-training-system.md)

```
EAR Ear
+-- Pitch ............ EAR-01 pitch matching, EAR-02 higher/lower
+-- Relative pitch ... EAR-03 S R G P vs drone, EAR-04 all shuddha swaras, EAR-05 komal/tivra, EAR-06 intervals
+-- Rhythm ........... EAR-07 rhythm dictation, EAR-11 identifying tala/meter
+-- Melody ........... EAR-08 melodic dictation, EAR-09 phrase memory, EAR-10 finding Sa
+-- Style ............ EAR-12 raga recognition, EAR-13 ornament recognition, EAR-14 chord quality
```
