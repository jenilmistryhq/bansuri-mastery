# D11 - ORN: Ornamentation

[Back to Knowledge Tree](../01-KNOWLEDGE-TREE.md) | Symbols: [Conventions](../05-CONVENTIONS-AND-NOTATION-KEY.md)

> **Sequencing rule (Recommendation):** no ornament is introduced before you can play the notes
> involved cleanly, in tune and in time. Ornaments built on unstable notes make habits that are
> hard to undo.

```
ORN Ornaments
+-- Concept .............. ORN-01 ornaments as grammar
+-- Glides ............... ORN-02 meend
+-- Grace and turns ...... ORN-03 kan, ORN-04 khatka and murki
+-- Oscillations ......... ORN-05 andolan, ORN-06 gamak, ORN-08 vibrato/kampan
+-- Style ................ ORN-07 zamzama (Enrich), ORN-09 ornament choice by style
```
