# D03 - TON: Tone Production

[Back to Knowledge Tree](../01-KNOWLEDGE-TREE.md) | Symbols: [Conventions](../05-CONVENTIONS-AND-NOTATION-KEY.md)

```
TON Tone
+-- Embouchure ............ TON-01 lip shape, TON-02 air direction/angle
+-- First and stable sound  TON-03 first sound, TON-04 sustain, TON-05 clarity
+-- Registers ............. TON-06 low register, TON-07 upper register
+-- Expression ............ TON-08 dynamics, TON-09 tone colour, TON-10 evenness
```

| ID | Subtopic | Understand / perform | Why it matters | Prereqs | Diff | Imp | Mastery test | Phase | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TON-01 | Embouchure | Form a small relaxed lip opening, place the lower lip so it covers roughly a quarter to a third of the blowing hole (Uncertain: exact coverage varies by player and flute) | Every note depends on it | BOD-03 | 1 | Core | Mirror check: centred opening, no puffed cheeks, relaxed corners | P1 | [L04](../lessons/L04-first-sound.md) |
| TON-02 | Air direction | Aim the air stream at the far edge of the blowing hole; roll the flute slightly in/out to find the clearest sound | Wrong angle gives airy or no sound | TON-01 | 1 | Core | Find the "sweet spot" within 10 s from a fresh start, 3 times | P1 | [L04](../lessons/L04-first-sound.md) |
| TON-03 | First sound | Produce a clear Sa (`XXX OOO`) | Easiest note; foundation for everything | TON-02 | 1 | Core | Sa sounds clearly 4 out of 5 attempts | P1 | [L04](../lessons/L04-first-sound.md) |
| TON-04 | Sustain | Hold a steady note for 4 s, then 8 s, then 12 s+ without wobble or pitch drift | Long tones build tone, breath and pitch control | TON-03, BRE-03 | 2 | Core | Sa, Re, Ga each held 12 s, tuner needle within +/-15 cents, no breaks | P1 | [L07](../lessons/L07-long-tones-tuner-and-drone.md) |
| TON-05 | Clarity | Reduce hiss (air noise) so the note is mostly tone | Professional sound; recordings expose airiness | TON-04 | 2 | High | Recording: notes judged "clear" on the tone rubric (score 3/4+) | P1-P2 | [L07](../lessons/L07-long-tones-tuner-and-drone.md) |
| TON-06 | Low register | Sound mandra Pa/Dha/Ni with slow, warm, wide air and perfect sealing | Mandra notes are characteristic of bansuri and needed in most songs | TON-04, BOD-04 | 2 | High | `.P` held 6 s clean, 5/5 attempts | P1-P2 | [L11](../lessons/L11-lower-notes-ni-dha-pa.md) |
| TON-07 | Upper register | Overblow to the 2nd register with faster, narrower air and smaller lip opening, not with force | Needed for madhya Pa-Dha-Ni and taar saptak | TON-04 | 2 | High | Jump `.P` to `P` and back 5 times cleanly | P2 | [L14](../lessons/L14-tivra-ma-and-madhya-pa-dha-ni.md) |
| TON-08 | Dynamics | Play soft and loud while keeping pitch steady (compensate by angle and air speed) | Expression; on flute, louder tends to go sharp and softer flat | TON-07, PIT-04 | 4 | High | Crescendo/decrescendo on Sa over 8 s with pitch within +/-15 cents | P3 | [L17](../lessons/L17-intonation-and-drone.md) |
| TON-09 | Tone colour | Vary between darker and brighter tone; use breath vibrato sparingly (Disputed: the amount of vibrato in Hindustani bansuri varies a lot between players) | Personal sound and stylistic fit | TON-08 | 4 | Medium | Play one phrase in 2 deliberate tone colours; listener can tell which is which | P4 | [L33](../lessons/L33-gamak-andolan-advanced-ornaments.md) |
| TON-10 | Evenness | Matching tone quality across mandra, madhya and taar | Phrases spanning octaves must sound like one voice | TON-06, TON-07, FNG-09 | 4 | High | Slow 2-octave scale recording rated even on the tone rubric | P4 | [L22](../lessons/L22-taar-saptak.md) |
