# Listening Library

Bansuri recordings for every raga in this curriculum, plus general listening. Researched and
verified 2026-10-29.

**Verification:** every YouTube link was checked twice through YouTube's oEmbed service, which
returns the video's exact title and channel. Titles below are copied from it. **Nobody
listened to these recordings for this curriculum:** sound quality and the accuracy of the
performances are **not assessed**. Lengths are as shown in YouTube search listings.

**Channel types:**

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Official** | The record label (Saregama, Music Today, T-Series), a festival (Darbar), a broadcaster (All India Radio) or the artist's own channel |
| **Topic** | A YouTube auto-generated channel carrying official label audio |
| **Organiser** | The channel of the concert's organiser |
| **Re-upload** | A third-party channel. It may disappear or be lower quality |
| **Unconfirmed** | Could not confirm whether the channel is official |

Recordings marked **SHORT** are 10 minutes or less: use these first. Long concerts are for
later study of structure.

## How to listen (Recommendation)

Listening is practice ([PRA-07](knowledge-tree/D19-PRA-practice.md), [L01](lessons/L01-how-to-practise.md) listening log).
Listen with **one question at a time**:

| Phase | Questions to listen with |
|---|---|
| 1-2 | Where does the flautist breathe? How long are the held notes? What does the tone sound like in the low notes? |
| 3 | Can I hear Sa? Which notes does the player keep returning to? Can I hear meend (glides) and kan (grace notes)? Where does the alap end and the composition start? |
| 4 | Can I sing the pakad when it appears? How are taans built? Where are the tihais? How does this player's raga differ from another player's? |
| 5 | How is the whole performance paced? How does the player build to the climax? What would I do differently? |

**Tip:** many recordings are not in C. Before playing along, find the performer's Sa ([L18](lessons/L18-song-learning-system.md) Step 2). The note names on your flute will differ, but the raga shapes stay the same.

## Gaps

- **Khamaj and Kafi:** official bansuri recordings are mostly light-classical (thumri, dhun) or
  variants (Manj Khamaj). That is authentic, but for the straight raga use R-L72 (Khamaj) and
  the raga card.
- **Several useful items are re-uploads** and may disappear. If a link dies, search the exact
  title and artist.
- **Titles without "flute":** the Topic-channel items for Hariprasad Chaurasia, Rakesh
  Chaurasia, Ronu Majumdar and Pravin Godkhindi are accepted because these artists are
  bansuri players.
