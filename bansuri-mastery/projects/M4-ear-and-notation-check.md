# M4 - Ear and Notation Check

[Projects](README.md) | Phase: [2](../phases/phase-2-core-technique.md) | Objectives: EAR-01..04, EAR-07..09, NOT-01..03, PIT-03, PIT-04, SNG-01 (introduction)

Use the self-recorded or partner method from [EX-EAR](../exercises/EX-EAR-ear-training-system.md), with material prepared **at least 2 days earlier**, or by someone else.

| # | Item | Pass criterion | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identify 20 single notes from `S R G P` against the drone | 18/20 | Yes |
| 2 | Identify 20 single notes from `S R G m P D N` against the drone | 16/20 | Yes |
| 3 | Rhythm dictation: 10 two-beat-group rhythms ([EX-RHY](../exercises/EX-RHY-rhythm-drills.md)) | 8/10 | Yes |
| 4 | Melodic dictation: 5 melodies of 5-6 notes in the middle octave | 4/5 exact | Yes |
| 5 | Phrase memory: hear 2 times, then play back 10 two-bar phrases | 8/10 | Yes |
| 6 | Tuning: hold each of `S G P` against the drone until it locks; record with the tuner visible | All within +/-10 cents | Yes |
| 7 | Song pass: one simple melody learned with the [L18](../lessons/L18-song-learning-system.md) process, notation written and verified | Notation matches the source when played | Yes |

## If you did not pass

| Failed item | Probable weakness | Targeted practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1, 2 | Not hearing notes in relation to Sa | Sing the note back and then sing down to Sa ("resolve to Sa") for every quiz item; [R-E02](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#73-ear-training) 10 min daily |
| 3 | Pulse not felt while listening | Tap the beat with your foot during all dictation; [TS-09](../troubleshooting/TS-09-rhythm-and-tempo.md) |
| 4, 5 | Short-term musical memory | Sing each phrase immediately after hearing it before writing or playing; [TS-10](../troubleshooting/TS-10-ear-and-memory.md) |
| 6 | Not hearing "beats" against the drone | [L17](../lessons/L17-intonation-and-drone.md) slow-bend exercise |
| 7 | Guessing notes instead of verifying | Re-read the "verify, don't guess" part of L18 |
