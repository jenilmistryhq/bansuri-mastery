# M3 - Full Middle Octave

[Projects](README.md) | Phase: [2](../phases/phase-2-core-technique.md) | Objectives: FNG-04, FNG-05, FNG-07, FNG-11, TON-07, PIT-08, RHY-05..07, RHY-14, THY-04

## Test (drone on C; tabla loop for items 4-5)

| # | Item | Pass criterion | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Octave slurs `.P -> P -> .P`, `.D -> D -> .D`, `.N -> N -> .N` | Each 5/5 clean with the same fingering | Yes |
| 2 | Kalyan scale `S R G M P D N S'` and back, 60 BPM | Clean, `M` with good tone | Yes |
| 3 | Bilawal (major) scale `S R G m P D N S'` and back, 60 BPM | `m` within +/-20 cents on the tuner (check on the recording or live) | Yes |
| 4 | Two alankars (full octave) over a **teentaal** loop, 2 notes per beat at 60 BPM | No drift; you start and end on sam | Yes |
| 5 | Two melodies (one with shuddha `m`, e.g. Twinkle) over a keherwa loop | Correct notes and rhythm, from memory | Yes |
| 6 | Recite teentaal with claps and waves, 2 cycles aloud then 2 silent, clap on sam | Lands exactly on sam | Yes |
| 7 | Articulation: `S S R R G G m m P` tongued at 80 BPM | Notes separated, tone unchanged | Yes |
| 8 | Rubric on item 5 | Tone 3+, Pitch 3+, Rhythm 3+ | Yes |
| 9 | Teacher check (in person or online, video) | Embouchure and posture approved or corrections noted | Strongly recommended |

## If you did not pass

| Failed item | Probable weakness | Targeted practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Over-blowing with force instead of faster, narrower air | [L14](../lessons/L14-tivra-ma-and-madhya-pa-dha-ni.md) section 4.2 (mandra Pa to madhya Pa); [TS-02](../troubleshooting/TS-02-squeaks-and-wrong-octave.md) |
| 2 (weak `M`) | Lip too open on the all-open note | Long tones on `M` with mirror; [TS-01](../troubleshooting/TS-01-no-sound-or-airy-tone.md) |
| 3 | Half-hole coverage inconsistent | [L16](../lessons/L16-shuddha-ma-and-half-holing.md) slide drill with tuner 5 min daily; [TS-05](../troubleshooting/TS-05-pitch-out-of-tune.md) |
| 4, 5, 6 | Weak internal pulse | [EX-RHY](../exercises/EX-RHY-rhythm-drills.md) tala recitation 10 min daily; play with the metronome muted every 4th bar |
| 7 | Tongue too heavy | [L13](../lessons/L13-articulation-tonguing-and-slurs.md) "light da" drill |
