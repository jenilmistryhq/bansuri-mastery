# M10 - Independent Musician

[Projects](README.md) | Phase: [5](../phases/phase-5-advanced-independence.md) | Objectives: RAG-12, RAG-13, RHY-13, IMP-07, CMP-05, ENS-05, PRF-07, PRA-08, FNG-10 | Lesson: [L37](../lessons/L37-full-raga-presentation-and-independence.md)

This milestone is a portfolio, assembled over months, not a single test day.

| # | Portfolio item | Pass criterion | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full raga presentation, 25-30 min (alap, jod, gat in two tempos, taans, closing tihai), live with a tabla player | Coherent structure; correct grammar; rubric average 3.5+; tabla player confirms clean coordination | Yes |
| 2 | A second full raga, 15+ min, including one heavy-komal raga (Bhairav, Bhairavi or Malkauns) | Intonation judged correct by a teacher or an experienced musician | Yes |
| 3 | Learn a song in an **unfamiliar style** by ear within 5 practice sessions, with accurate notation | Play-along test passes; notation verified | Yes |
| 4 | Original 3-5 minute piece (composed and arranged), recorded with a drone/tabla or other instruments | Clear structure; you can explain the choices | Yes |
| 5 | Accompany a singer you have not rehearsed with, after hearing the song once or twice | Supportive, in key, no clashes | Yes |
| 6 | A self-designed 4-week plan for a new goal, carried out with a log and a before/after recording | Measurable improvement on the targeted skill | Yes |
| 7 | Diagnosis log: 5 problems you diagnosed and fixed yourself, with evidence | Each entry has symptom, hypothesis, test, fix, verification | Yes |
| 8 | Similar-raga demonstration: play contrasting phrases for 2 pairs (e.g. Bhupali/Deshkar) | Listener identifies each correctly | Yes |
| 9 | Optional: circular breathing, 3rd-register chart, Carnatic piece | | Enrich |

## If you did not pass

Use the methods you learned in [L37](../lessons/L37-full-raga-presentation-and-independence.md): identify the weakest item, diagnose it, design your own plan, run it, and retest. Deciding what to do next is now part of the test.
