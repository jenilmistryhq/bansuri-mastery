# M2 - First Melody in Time

[Projects](README.md) | Phase: [1](../phases/phase-1-foundations.md) | Objectives: FNG-02, FNG-03, FNG-06, TON-04..06, RHY-01..04, NOT-01..03, SNG-02, PRF-01, PRF-02

## Test

| # | Item | Pass criterion | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Long tones `.P .D .N S R G`, with drone | Each held 8 s, clear; `.P` sounds at the first attempt in at least 4 of 5 tries | Yes |
| 2 | `S .N .D .P .D .N S R G R S` at 60 BPM | 2 clean repetitions in a row | Yes |
| 3 | Two alankars from [L12](../lessons/L12-alankars.md) (6-note set), ascending and descending, 60 BPM | No missed notes; stays with the metronome | Yes |
| 4 | Two melodies from [EX-MEL Level 2](../exercises/EX-MEL-practice-melodies.md) **from memory**, 60 BPM | Correct notes and rhythm; breaths only at breath marks | Yes |
| 5 | Sight-reading: play an unseen 8-beat line from [EX-NOT](../exercises/EX-NOT-notation-drills.md) Level 1 after 1 minute of silent reading | At most 1 error | Yes |
| 6 | Notation writing: write the melody from item 4 from memory, with rhythm marks | Matches the source | Yes |
| 7 | Rubric on item 4 | Tone at least 3, Rhythm at least 3, no category below 2 | Yes |

## What success sounds like

The melody sounds like music, not like a series of separate attempts. The beat stays steady
even on the low notes, there are no squeaks, and breaths do not interrupt phrases.

## If you did not pass

| Failed item | Probable weakness | Targeted practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (`.P`) | Leaking finger seal or air too fast | [TS-03](../troubleshooting/TS-03-low-notes-wont-sound.md) seal test; slow-air drill 5 min daily |
| 2, 3 | Uneven fingers or rushing | Metronome ladder from 40 BPM ([L01](../lessons/L01-how-to-practise.md)); [TS-06](../troubleshooting/TS-06-finger-leaks-and-transitions.md), [TS-09](../troubleshooting/TS-09-rhythm-and-tempo.md) |
| 4 | Memory or phrase planning | Sing the melody in sargam before playing; mark breaths; [TS-10](../troubleshooting/TS-10-ear-and-memory.md) |
| 5, 6 | Notation fluency | 5 min [EX-NOT](../exercises/EX-NOT-notation-drills.md) daily for a week |
| 7 (Tone) | Airy tone under pressure | Long-tone block extended to 15 min; [TS-01](../troubleshooting/TS-01-no-sound-or-airy-tone.md) |
