# M7 - First Recital

[Projects](README.md) | Phase: [3](../phases/phase-3-musicianship.md) | Objectives: RAG-02, RAG-03, ORN-02..04, FNG-09, SNG-03, SNG-04, IMP-02, CMP-01, PRF-04..06 | Prep: [L35](../lessons/L35-performance-and-interpretation.md) (read the preparation sections early)

## The programme (10-12 minutes, to a small live audience of at least 2 people, recorded)

| Order | Piece | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Raga Yaman** (4-5 min) | Short alap-style opening (1-2 min, with meend and kan in appropriate places), then the [L21](../lessons/L21-raga-yaman.md) composition with a tabla loop, 3+ cycles |
| 2 | **Bhupali** composition (2 min) | With tabla loop |
| 3 | **A bhajan** (2 min) | With meend; played from memory |
| 4 | **A film song** learned by ear (2 min) | Upper-octave passage included if the song has one |
| 5 | **Your original 16-beat melody** (1 min) | Introduce it in one sentence |

## Criteria

| # | Criterion | Required |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Whole programme from memory, no stopping (mistakes are allowed; recovery counts) | Yes |
| 2 | Rubric average 3+ across the programme; no category below 2 in any piece | Yes |
| 3 | Taar saptak notes in tune (+/-20 cents on the recording) | Yes |
| 4 | Ornaments (meend, kan, at least one murki) clean where used | Yes |
| 5 | Three mock performances done and logged before the recital | Yes |
| 6 | Written self-review within 2 days (what went well, 3 things to improve) | Yes |
| 7 | Teacher feedback on the recording | Strongly recommended |

## If you did not pass

| Failed criterion | Probable weakness | Targeted practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Memory or nerves | Starting-points memory test, Step 2 of [L35](../lessons/L35-performance-and-interpretation.md); 3 more mock performances with "keep going" rule |
| 2 | One weak piece pulling the average down | Isolate that piece; 2 weeks of focused practice; retest that piece only |
| 3 | Upper-octave intonation | [L22](../lessons/L22-taar-saptak.md) octave pairs with tuner; [TS-04](../troubleshooting/TS-04-high-notes.md) |
| 4 | Ornaments added before secure | Remove the ornament, re-drill it in isolation ([L23](../lessons/L23-meend.md), [L24](../lessons/L24-kan-khatka-murki.md)), add it back |
