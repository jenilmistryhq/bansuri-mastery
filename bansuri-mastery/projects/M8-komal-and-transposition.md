# M8 - Komal Notes, Transposition and Composition

[Projects](README.md) | Phase: [4](../phases/phase-4-intermediate-artistry.md) | Objectives: FNG-08, FNG-15, PIT-08, EAR-05, TRN-02..05, RAG-05 or RAG-06, CMP-02, CMP-03, NOT-07

| # | Item | Pass criterion | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Chromatic scale `S r R g G m M P d D n N S'` up and down, slowly with drone | Every note within +/-20 cents; clean | Yes |
| 2 | Each komal note held 4 s | Within +/-15 cents, 3 times in a row | Yes |
| 3 | Aroh, avroh, pakad and a composition in one komal raga (Khamaj, Des, Kafi, Bhimpalasi or Bageshri) | Correct notes; Pitch 3+ | Yes |
| 4 | One melody played with Sa = C, then with Pa as Sa (Sa = G), then with Ma as Sa (Sa = F), on your C flute | Correct in all three; written notation for each | Yes |
| 5 | Transpose a given 8-bar sargam notation into another Sa and convert it to Western staff (or the reverse) | No errors | Yes |
| 6 | Ear: EX-EAR Level 6 (12-note identification) | 17/20 | Yes |
| 7 | Original 16-bar melody with labelled phrase structure **and** an original sthayi in Bhupali or Yaman in teentaal | Notated, performed with a loop, recorded | Yes |

## If you did not pass

| Failed item | Probable weakness | Targeted practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1, 2 | Half-hole control | [L25](../lessons/L25-komal-swaras-and-12-notes.md) chart verification; tuner slides 10 min daily; try verified alternate fingerings (FNG-15) |
| 3 | Raga grammar with komal notes | Listen to the raga daily; sing the pakad before playing |
| 4, 5 | Interval thinking | [EX-THY](../exercises/EX-THY-theory-quizzes.md) transposition quiz; write the mapping table from [L26](../lessons/L26-keys-transposition-and-limits.md) by hand |
| 6 | Komal/tivra ear | Level 6 daily with sing-and-resolve-to-Sa method |
| 7 | Phrase design | [L30](../lessons/L30-composition-foundations.md) question/answer exercise; analyse 3 melodies you love |
