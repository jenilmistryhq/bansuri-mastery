# M1 - First Stable Tone

[Projects](README.md) | Phase: [1](../phases/phase-1-foundations.md) | Objectives: TON-01..04, BOD-01, BOD-03, BOD-05, PIT-02, FNG-02

## Test (record everything; drone on C for items 2-4)

| # | Item | Pass criterion | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Posture video (30 s, side view) | Tall relaxed spine, shoulders down, flute steady, matches the [L02](../lessons/L02-posture-and-holding.md) checklist | Yes |
| 2 | 5 fresh starts on Sa | At least 4 of 5 start within 1 s with a clear tone | Yes |
| 3 | Long tone Sa | Held 8 s; tuner stays within +/-20 cents; no breaks or cracks | Yes |
| 4 | Long tones Re and Ga | Each held 6 s, clear | Yes |
| 5 | `S R G R S` at 50 BPM, 1 note per beat | 3 clean repetitions in a row, no blips between notes | Yes |
| 6 | Rubric | Tone at least 2 | Yes |

## What success sounds like

A clear, steady note with only a little breath noise around it. It starts without a
"chiff" or delay and stops cleanly. The tuner needle wobbles slightly but does not drift.

## If you did not pass

| Failed item | Probable underlying weakness | Targeted practice (5-10 days) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tension or unstable grip | L02 tension scan before every block; 2-min mirror holding drill daily; [TS-08](../troubleshooting/TS-08-body-pain-and-tension.md) |
| 2 | Embouchure not found reliably | [L04](../lessons/L04-first-sound.md) Step 4.5 (take the flute away, then find the sweet spot again within 10 s), 20 times per day; [TS-01](../troubleshooting/TS-01-no-sound-or-airy-tone.md) |
| 3 | Unsteady air support | Hiss test ([L03](../lessons/L03-breathing-basics.md)) 3 x 20 s; long tones with tuner 3 x 5 min; [EX-TON](../exercises/EX-TON-tone-and-breath-drills.md) week-1 routine |
| 4 | Lip opening changes with fingering | Alternate S-R-S-G-S long tones; keep the mouth still in the mirror |
| 5 | Fingers move at different times | [L09](../lessons/L09-fingering-sa-re-ga.md) "finger together" drill at 40 BPM; [TS-06](../troubleshooting/TS-06-finger-leaks-and-transitions.md) |
