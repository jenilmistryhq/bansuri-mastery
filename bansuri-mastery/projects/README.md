# Milestones and Projects

Milestones are the **only** proof of progress in this curriculum. Watching videos or finishing
lessons does not count. Each milestone is a practical test with measurable criteria.

## How every milestone works

1. **Rehearse** the test 2-3 times in the week before (record these too).
2. **Test conditions:** warm up for at most 10 minutes, then record the test **in one take
   per item** (2 attempts allowed per item; the better one counts). No editing.
3. **Score** with the rubric from [L08](../lessons/L08-recording-and-self-assessment.md)
   (Tone, Pitch, Rhythm, Transitions/Technique, Expression; 1-4 each). **Wait at least one
   day before scoring.** You hear your own playing more accurately after a break.
4. **Check the criteria** in the milestone file. Every criterion marked **Required** must pass.
5. **Record the result** in [03-PROGRESS-TRACKER.md](../03-PROGRESS-TRACKER.md).
6. **If you fail**, use the milestone's "If you did not pass" table. It names the probable
   underlying weakness and the targeted practice. Do it for 5-10 practice days, then retest
   **only the failed items**. Failing is information, not a judgement.

**Second listener (Recommendation):** from M3 onward, ask someone (ideally a teacher; otherwise
any attentive listener) to score the recording too. If your score and theirs differ by more
than 1 point in a category, trust the lower one and investigate.
