# M9 - Intermediate Recital and Accompaniment

[Projects](README.md) | Phase: [4](../phases/phase-4-intermediate-artistry.md) | Objectives: RAG-09..11, RHY-08..11, ORN-05, ORN-06, ENS-02, ENS-03, IMP-04..06, SNG-05, SNG-07, PRF-05, PRF-06, PRF-08

## Part A - Raga presentation (15 minutes, live audience, recorded)

One raga from Yaman, Bhupali, or a Phase 4 raga:

| Section | Approximate time | Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| Alap | 5 min | Gradual development from mandra/madhya to taar following the [L31](../lessons/L31-alap-and-swar-vistar.md) stages; meend, kan, andolan where appropriate |
| Bandish/gat in teentaal (or jhaptaal/rupak) | 8 min | Sthayi and antara; at least 5 taans (dugun or chaugun) returning cleanly to the mukhda; at least 2 tihais landing on sam |
| Ending | 2 min | Faster section or drut composition, ending with a tihai |

## Part B - Accompaniment (recorded)

Accompany a singer (live, or a recorded vocal track) in **two songs**: one bhajan and one film
song or ghazal. Match their key, play an introduction, fills between lines and interludes,
and do **not** play over the words.

## Part C - Style

Play one folk tune or dhun in a convincing folk style ([L36](../lessons/L36-style-adaptation.md)).

## Criteria

| # | Criterion | Required |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Raga grammar: no forbidden notes; characteristic phrases present (checklist in L31) | Yes |
| 2 | Rhythm: no lost sam; all tihais land correctly | Yes |
| 3 | Rubric average 3+ in Part A; Expression 3+ | Yes |
| 4 | Singer (or an attentive listener) rates the accompaniment "supportive, not intrusive" | Yes |
| 5 | Self-review comparing your alap with a master recording of the same raga | Yes |
| 6 | Teacher assessment | Strongly recommended |

## If you did not pass

| Failed criterion | Probable weakness | Targeted practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Raga not internalised | 2 weeks of listening plus singing the raga before playing; copy 2 phrases daily from a master recording |
| 2 | Layakari or tihai arithmetic | [L32](../lessons/L32-bandish-taans-layakari-tihai.md) Step 6 (tihai mathematics); recite before playing |
| 3 (Expression) | Playing "correctly" but flatly | [L35](../lessons/L35-performance-and-interpretation.md) phrase-arc exercise; dynamics drills |
| 4 | Overplaying | [L34](../lessons/L34-accompaniment-and-ensemble.md): mark where the singer breathes and where nobody sings, and play only there |
