# M6 - A Song Learned Entirely by Ear

[Projects](README.md) | Phase: [3](../phases/phase-3-musicianship.md) | Objectives: SNG-01, SNG-04, SNG-06, SNG-09, EAR-08..10, NOT-03, NOT-05, TRN-01, TRN-06 | Lesson: [L18](../lessons/L18-song-learning-system.md)

**Choose** a song (film song or bhajan) that you have **never seen notated**, that you like,
and whose melody fits your flute's range (check with L18 and [L26](../lessons/L26-keys-transposition-and-limits.md)). At least 2 sections
(for example mukhda and one antara), at least 60 seconds of melody.

| # | Item | Pass criterion | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Completed song-learning worksheet (L18) | All 12 steps documented, including how each uncertain note was **verified** (not guessed) | Yes |
| 2 | Sa of the song identified and stated as a pitch (e.g. "Sa = A") | Confirmed with drone/tuner | Yes |
| 3 | Written notation with rhythm marks, sections and breath marks | When you play exactly what is written, it matches the recording | Yes |
| 4 | Play-along recording with the original (pitch-shifted to your flute's key if needed) | No audible note clashes; timing aligned | Yes |
| 5 | Solo performance from memory at the original tempo | Rubric: Pitch 3+, Rhythm 3+, Expression 2+ | Yes |
| 6 | Reflection: 3 hardest notes/passages and how you solved them | Written | Yes |

**Time limit:** none. Most learners need 1-3 weeks for their first full song by ear.

## If you did not pass

| Failed item | Probable weakness | Targeted practice |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Finding the tonic | L18 Step 2 (find the tonal centre) on 5 more songs; [EX-EAR](../exercises/EX-EAR-ear-training-system.md) Level 10 preparation |
| 3 | Guessing; rhythm not notated | Slow the recording to 50-70% ([R-A14](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#72-tuner-metronome-recording-slow-down)); notate in 2-bar chunks; tap the beat while listening |
| 4 | Key mismatch or a wrong octave | Re-check Sa; pitch-shift the track ([L26](../lessons/L26-keys-transposition-and-limits.md)) |
| 5 | Memory or the style's feel | Sing along with the recording 5 times before playing; [L36](../lessons/L36-style-adaptation.md) preview for film articulation |
