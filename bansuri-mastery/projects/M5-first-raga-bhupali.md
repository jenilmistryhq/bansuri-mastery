# M5 - First Raga: Bhupali

[Projects](README.md) | Phase: [3](../phases/phase-3-musicianship.md) | Objectives: THY-07, THY-08, RAG-01, RAG-02, RHY-07, RHY-14, PIT-03 | Lesson: [L20](../lessons/L20-raga-bhupali.md) | Card: [ragas/bhupali](../ragas/bhupali.md)

| # | Item | Pass criterion | Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Explain Bhupali verbally (1 min): thaat, notes, varjit notes, vadi/samvadi, time, pakad | All elements correct | Yes |
| 2 | Aroh and avroh with drone, slow and then at 2 notes per beat (60 BPM) | Clean, in tune, no Ma or Ni anywhere | Yes |
| 3 | Pakad / characteristic phrases (from L20), 3 times | Recognisably Bhupali (compare with [R-L03](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#9-listening-library)) | Yes |
| 4 | 3 minutes of swar vistar (slow exploration) with drone | Obeys the raga's notes; rests on Sa, Ga or Pa; no random "scale running" | Yes |
| 5 | The L20 composition with a teentaal loop: sthayi 3 cycles | Starts and ends correctly relative to sam; no time drift | Yes |
| 6 | Listening: name the stages (alap, composition, taans) of a 10-minute recorded raga performance, with timestamps | Main boundaries within about 30 s | Yes |
| 7 | Rubric on items 4 and 5 | Pitch 3+, Rhythm 3+, Expression 2+ | Yes |
| 8 | Teacher or experienced listener feedback on item 4 | Comments recorded | Strongly recommended |

## If you did not pass

| Failed item | Probable weakness | Targeted practice |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Theory not consolidated | Re-read [L19](../lessons/L19-what-is-a-raga.md); [EX-THY](../exercises/EX-THY-theory-quizzes.md) raga quiz |
| 3, 4 | Thinking in scales, not phrases | Listen to the reference daily; copy one phrase per day by ear; [L31](../lessons/L31-alap-and-swar-vistar.md) preview |
| 5 | Mukhda/sam placement | Count aloud with the loop; [L15](../lessons/L15-taal-basics.md) and RHY-09 drills in [EX-RHY](../exercises/EX-RHY-rhythm-drills.md) |
| 6 | Not enough structured listening | 3 guided listening sessions per week with [R-T07](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#5-theory-and-raga-references) |
