# Phase 4 - Intermediate Artistry (about months 10-20)

[Mastery Map](../00-MASTERY-MAP.md) | Previous: [Phase 3](phase-3-musicianship.md) | Next: [Phase 5](phase-5-advanced-independence.md)

**Typical duration:** 8-12 months. **From this phase on, regular guidance from a live teacher
matters more than ever.** Alap, taans and gamak are hard to self-assess from text. Plan
monthly lessons if you can ([R-C01](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#4-courses-and-teachers-paid-or-structured)-[R-C03](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#4-courses-and-teachers-paid-or-structured)).
**Goal:** all 12 notes; several new ragas; transposition; alap; bandish with taans and tihais;
advanced ornaments; Western notation; accompanying singers; stylistic flexibility; confident
performance.
**Lessons:** L25, L26, L28, L31-L36. **Milestones:** [M8](../projects/M8-komal-and-transposition.md) (about month 14), [M9](../projects/M9-intermediate-recital.md) (about month 20).

## Session template (60 min; on some days, a split 2 x 30 min works better)

| Block | Min | Content |
|---|---|---|
| W | 5 | Warm-up |
| T | 8-10 | Long tones and swells in all three octaves; komal notes against the drone |
| K | 15 | Current technique (komal notes, speed, gamak) |
| M | 20 | Raga (alap, bandish, taans) or song/accompaniment project |
| E | 8 | Ear (Levels 6-9), transcription, theory |
| R | 2-5 | Record, log; full rubric once a week |

**Weekly rotation (Recommendation):** 3 raga days, 2 song/accompaniment days, 1 creative day
(improvisation/composition), 1 review day (spaced repertoire review, listening, reflection).

## Modules

### 4A - Komal swaras and the 12 notes (months 10-12)

- [L25](../lessons/L25-komal-swaras-and-12-notes.md). Objectives: FNG-08, FNG-15, PIT-08, EAR-05.
- Watch [R-V05](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#31-structured-beginner-series), [R-V14](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#32-single-technique-demonstrations), [R-V15](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#32-single-technique-demonstrations).
- Build your verified komal chart. Chromatic scale slowly with the tuner.
- Ear: EX-EAR Level 6 (komal/tivra recognition).

### 4B - New ragas (months 11-20, rolling)

Order (Recommendation): [Khamaj](../ragas/khamaj.md) or [Des](../ragas/des.md) (komal Ni) ->
[Kafi](../ragas/kafi.md) / [Bhimpalasi](../ragas/bhimpalasi.md) / [Bageshri](../ragas/bageshri.md) (komal Ga and Ni) ->
[Shivranjani](../ragas/shivranjani.md) / [Pahadi](../ragas/pahadi.md) (film and folk links) -> [Hamsadhwani](../ragas/hamsadhwani.md).
Aim for one raga every 6-8 weeks: aroh/avroh/pakad, listening, swar vistar, one composition.
Objectives: RAG-05, RAG-06, RAG-08.

### 4C - Keys, transposition and limits (months 12-14)

- [L26](../lessons/L26-keys-transposition-and-limits.md). Objectives: TRN-02, TRN-03, TRN-04, TRN-05, INS-08, INS-10.
- Play known melodies with Pa-as-Sa and Ma-as-Sa on your C flute; transpose notations.
- Decide whether and when to buy a second flute (Recommendation in L26).

### Milestone M8 (about month 14)

[M8 - Komal notes and transposition](../projects/M8-komal-and-transposition.md).

### 4D - Western notation and theory (months 12-15, E-block)

- [L28](../lessons/L28-western-notation-basics.md). Objectives: NOT-06, NOT-07, NOT-10, THY-05, THY-06, THY-10, THY-11, RHY-12.
- Use [R-T11](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#5-theory-and-raga-references) musictheory.net for drills.

### 4E - Alap and swar vistar (months 13-17)

- [L31](../lessons/L31-alap-and-swar-vistar.md). Objectives: RAG-09, IMP-04, THY-09, THY-12.
- Start with Yaman, then Bhupali, then one Phase 4 raga.

### 4F - Bandish, taans, layakari, tihai (months 14-20)

- [L32](../lessons/L32-bandish-taans-layakari-tihai.md). Objectives: RAG-10, RAG-11, RHY-08..11, FNG-13, IMP-05.
- Learn one published bandish ([R-T01](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#5-theory-and-raga-references), [R-T04](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#5-theory-and-raga-references)) and compose one of your own (CMP-03, [L30](../lessons/L30-composition-foundations.md)).
- Double tonguing for fast passages ([L13](../lessons/L13-articulation-tonguing-and-slurs.md) later section, FNG-12).

### 4G - Gamak, andolan, advanced ornaments (months 16-20)

- [L33](../lessons/L33-gamak-andolan-advanced-ornaments.md). Objectives: ORN-05, ORN-06, ORN-08, TON-09, EAR-13.
- Watch [R-V23](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#32-single-technique-demonstrations), [R-V24](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#32-single-technique-demonstrations). Read [R-W02](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#2-written-lessons-and-fingering-charts).

### 4H - Accompaniment, style, performance (months 15-20)

- [L34](../lessons/L34-accompaniment-and-ensemble.md), [L36](../lessons/L36-style-adaptation.md), [L35](../lessons/L35-performance-and-interpretation.md).
- Objectives: ENS-02..04, IMP-06, SNG-05, SNG-07, ORN-09, PRF-04..06, PRF-08, BOD-02.
- Find a singer (friend, family member, or a recorded vocal track) and accompany 2 songs.
- Learn one folk tune and one dhun.
- Do 3 mock performances before M9.

### Milestone M9 (about month 20)

[M9 - Intermediate recital](../projects/M9-intermediate-recital.md).

## Phase 4 exit gate

- [ ] M8 and M9 passed
- [ ] EX-EAR Levels 6-8 passed; Level 9 started
- [ ] At least 5 ragas at "can present for 5+ minutes" level
- [ ] Accompanied a singer in at least 2 songs
- [ ] At least 1 original bandish and 3 original melodies
