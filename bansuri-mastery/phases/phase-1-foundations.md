# Phase 1 - Foundations (about Weeks 1-6)

[Mastery Map](../00-MASTERY-MAP.md) | Previous: [Phase 0](phase-0-setup.md) | Next: [Phase 2](phase-2-core-technique.md)

**Typical duration:** 5-8 weeks at 45-60 min/day. This is an estimate, not a deadline.
**Goal:** a relaxed posture; a clear, steady tone; the notes `.P .D .N S R G` played cleanly;
steady pulse; reading and writing simple sargam; first melodies.
**Lessons:** L01-L12. **Milestones:** [M1](../projects/M1-first-stable-tone.md) (about Day 14) and [M2](../projects/M2-first-melody.md) (about Day 42).

## How to use the day-by-day plan

- **Day numbers are practice days, not calendar days.** If you miss a day, continue from
  where you stopped. Do not "catch up" by doubling a session.
- **Lips tire quickly at the start.** In Week 1 total blowing time is capped at about
  15-20 minutes per day. The rest of the session is off-flute work (rhythm, singing,
  listening, theory). This is deliberate.
- If a day's "pass check" fails, **repeat that day** (or the drill) before moving on.
- Every 7th day is a lighter **review day**.
- Session blocks (from [L01](../lessons/L01-how-to-practise.md)):
  **W** = warm-up, **T** = tone, **K** = technique (new skill), **M** = music (melodies),
  **E** = ear/rhythm/theory, **R** = record and log.

## Weeks 5-6 - Consolidation and the first real melody

From here the plan uses a **daily rotation** instead of fixed days, because the content is now
mostly practice of known skills. Do the rotation for 12 practice days, then a review day,
then M2.

**Daily template (50-55 min):**

| Block | Minutes | Content |
|---|---|---|
| W | 5 | Warm-up (L01) and tension scan |
| T | 12 | Long tones on `.P .D .N S R G`, 8-10 s each, with drone; one note per day gets a tuner check |
| K | 12 | Alankars from L12 Versions A and B on a metronome ladder: target 60 -> 72 BPM, 1 note per beat, then 2 notes per beat at 50 BPM |
| M | 12 | EX-MEL Level 2: learn one new melody every 3 days; keep the old ones in rotation |
| E | 10 | Alternate days: (a) EX-EAR Level 2 + 5 min singing sargam with drone; (b) EX-RHY + L10 writing drill |
| R | 4 | Record one item; rubric score; log; listening for the evening |

**Spaced review in this period:** every 3rd day, replace 6 minutes of K with the oldest
melody or alankar you have not played for a week.

**Day 41: review day.** **Day 42: [M2 - First melody](../projects/M2-first-melody.md).**
If M2 fails, the M2 file tells you what to practise for the failed criterion. Retest after
5-7 more practice days.

## Phase 1 exit gate

- [ ] M1 and M2 passed
- [ ] Rubric scores at least 3 for Tone and Rhythm on the M2 recording
- [ ] Practice log kept 5+ days per week
- [ ] No unresolved pain or discomfort ([TS-08](../troubleshooting/TS-08-body-pain-and-tension.md))
