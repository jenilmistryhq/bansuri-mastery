# Phase 3 - Musicianship (about Weeks 19-44, months 5-10)

[Mastery Map](../00-MASTERY-MAP.md) | Previous: [Phase 2](phase-2-core-technique.md) | Next: [Phase 4](phase-4-intermediate-artistry.md)

**Typical duration:** 5-7 months. Several modules run at the same time, so the session
template rotates content.
**Goal:** your first two ragas, the upper octave, the three basic ornaments, learning real songs
by ear, Bhatkhande notation, and first steps in improvisation and composition, ending with
your first recital.
**Lessons:** L19-L24, L27, L29, L30 (and the range sections of L26).
**Milestones:** [M5](../projects/M5-first-raga-bhupali.md) (about Week 26), [M6](../projects/M6-song-by-ear.md) (about Week 32), [M7](../projects/M7-recital-one.md) (about Week 44).

## Session template (55-60 min)

| Block | Min | Content |
|---|---|---|
| W | 5 | Warm-up |
| T | 10 | Long tones with drone, rotating mandra/madhya/taar; 2x per week, swells |
| K | 15 | Current technique module (taar saptak, meend, kan/murki) |
| M | 15 | Current raga or song module |
| E | 10 | Ear level work + notation/theory |
| R | 3-5 | Record, rubric, log |

**Weekly rhythm (Recommendation):**

| Day | Emphasis in M block |
|---|---|
| 1, 3, 5 | Raga work (current raga) |
| 2, 4 | Song by ear (current song) |
| 6 | Improvisation/composition prompt ([EX-IMP](../exercises/EX-IMP-improv-and-composition-prompts.md)) |
| 7 | Review day: repertoire rotation (spaced review), listening 15 min, weekly reflection |

Daily listening outside practice (10-20 min, any time): raga recordings from the
[listening library](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#9-listening-library) and songs you plan to learn. Log them (PRA-07).

## Modules

### 3A - What a raga is, and Raga Bhupali (Weeks 19-26)

- **Lessons:** [L19](../lessons/L19-what-is-a-raga.md), then [L20](../lessons/L20-raga-bhupali.md). Card: [ragas/bhupali](../ragas/bhupali.md).
- **Objectives:** THY-07, THY-08, RAG-01, RAG-02, RAG-14, PIT-06 (introduction).
- **Listen:** [R-L03](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#9-listening-library) (The Raga Guide) and the L20 list. Use [R-T01](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#5-theory-and-raga-references) to look up the raga.
- **Sequence:** week 19-20 raga concept and listening; 21-22 aroh/avroh/pakad and swar vistar
  phrases; 23-25 the L20 composition in teentaal with a tabla loop; 26 **M5**.

### 3B - Taar saptak (Weeks 21-28, parallel with 3A)

- **Lesson:** [L22](../lessons/L22-taar-saptak.md). Objectives: FNG-09, FNG-14, PIT-07, TON-10 (start).
- **Watch:** [R-V12](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#32-single-technique-demonstrations), [R-V04](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#31-structured-beginner-series).
- **Practise:** K-block. Build your own verified upper-octave chart (tuner). Octave leap drills.
- **Gate:** 2-octave scale `.P` to `G'` at 60 BPM; octave pairs within +/-15 cents.

### 3C - Learning songs by ear for real (Weeks 23-32)

- **Lessons:** [L18](../lessons/L18-song-learning-system.md) (full method), range check in [L26](../lessons/L26-keys-transposition-and-limits.md) section on your flute's range.
- **Objectives:** SNG-01, SNG-03, SNG-04, SNG-06, SNG-09, EAR-10, TRN-01, TRN-06, NOT-05.
- **Practise:** one song per 2-3 weeks, alternating a bhajan and a film song. Choose songs
  whose range fits `.P` to about `G'`: start with Stage 1 of the [song suggestions](../09-SONG-SUGGESTIONS.md),
  then Stage 2. Write and verify the notation for each.
- **Milestone:** **M6** at about Week 32: a song you have never seen notated, learned by ear
  end-to-end.

### 3D - Meend, then kan, khatka and murki (Weeks 27-36)

- **Lessons:** [L23](../lessons/L23-meend.md) (Weeks 27-31), [L24](../lessons/L24-kan-khatka-murki.md) (Weeks 31-36).
- **Objectives:** ORN-01, ORN-02, ORN-03, ORN-04.
- **Read/watch:** [R-W02](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#2-written-lessons-and-fingering-charts); [R-V22](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#32-single-technique-demonstrations), [R-V29](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#32-single-technique-demonstrations), [R-V26](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#32-single-technique-demonstrations)-[R-V28](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#32-single-technique-demonstrations), [R-V25](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#32-single-technique-demonstrations) overview.
- **Apply:** add meend to your bhajans; kan and murki in Bhupali and in one film song.
- **Rule:** an ornament is only added to a piece after it passes its isolated drill.

### 3E - Raga Yaman (Weeks 29-40)

- **Lesson:** [L21](../lessons/L21-raga-yaman.md). Card: [ragas/yaman](../ragas/yaman.md). Objective: RAG-03.
- **Listen:** [R-L01](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#9-listening-library), [R-L02](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#9-listening-library). Lesson-style: [R-V31](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#32-single-technique-demonstrations) (check before use).
- **Sequence:** characteristic movements (`.N R G`, `M D N`); swar vistar; L21 composition;
  2-3 minutes of slow exploration with meend and kan in the right places.

### 3F - Bhatkhande notation (Weeks 28-31)

- **Lesson:** [L27](../lessons/L27-bhatkhande-notation.md). Objectives: NOT-04, NOT-05.
- **Practise:** transcribe one printed bandish (for example from [R-T01](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#5-theory-and-raga-references) or [R-T04](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#5-theory-and-raga-references)) into ASCII and play it.
  Find and fix 3 errors in an online song notation ([R-S01](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#8-song-notation-sources)) using the recording.

### 3G - Improvisation and composition foundations (Weeks 31-44)

- **Lessons:** [L29](../lessons/L29-improvisation-foundations.md), [L30](../lessons/L30-composition-foundations.md).
- **Objectives:** IMP-01, IMP-02, IMP-03, CMP-01, CMP-02.
- **Practise:** Day 6 of each week, plus 5 min at the end of raga sessions: call-and-response,
  3-note then pentatonic improvising over the drone, variations of known melodies, one 16-beat
  original melody every 2 weeks.

### 3H - Optional: Raga Durga (Weeks 38-44)

- Card: [ragas/durga](../ragas/durga.md). Objective: RAG-04. Practises shuddha Ma (half-hole)
  in a raga. Do it only if 3A-3E are on track.

### Rhythm and ear track (all of Phase 3)

| Weeks | Ear ([EX-EAR](../exercises/EX-EAR-ear-training-system.md)) | Rhythm ([EX-RHY](../exercises/EX-RHY-rhythm-drills.md)) / theory |
|---|---|---|
| 19-26 | Pass Level 4; begin Level 5 (intervals) | Dadra/keherwa songs; identifying tala by ear (EAR-11) |
| 27-34 | Level 5; Level 7 (melodic dictation) begins with 8-note melodies | Rupak, Jhaptaal recitation (RHY-08); starting compositions on the right beat (RHY-09) |
| 35-44 | Level 7 to 16-note melodies; phrase memory with raga phrases | Western meters and syncopation in film songs (RHY-12) |

### Milestone M7 - First recital (about Week 44)

[M7 - Recital one](../projects/M7-recital-one.md): 10-12 minutes for a small audience.
**Strongly recommended:** at least one teacher check in Weeks 36-42.

## Phase 3 exit gate

- [ ] M5, M6 and M7 passed
- [ ] EX-EAR Level 5 passed; Level 7 with 8-note melodies at 4/5
- [ ] Repertoire: 2 ragas, at least 3 bhajans, at least 3 film songs, 2 original melodies
- [ ] Rubric: Tone, Pitch and Rhythm at least 3 on the M7 recording
