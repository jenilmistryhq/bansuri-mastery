# Phase 2 - Core Technique (about Weeks 7-18)

[Mastery Map](../00-MASTERY-MAP.md) | Previous: [Phase 1](phase-1-foundations.md) | Next: [Phase 3](phase-3-musicianship.md)

**Typical duration:** 10-14 weeks. Overblowing and half-holes are where many learners slow
down. That is normal ([TS-11](../troubleshooting/TS-11-practice-plateaus-and-motivation.md)).
**Goal:** the full middle octave (both Ma), clean articulation, tala basics, intonation with a
drone, and the song-learning method. Ear training reaches all shuddha swaras.
**Lessons:** L13-L18. **Milestones:** [M3](../projects/M3-middle-octave.md) (about Week 13), [M4](../projects/M4-ear-and-notation-check.md) (about Week 18).

## Session template (55-60 min)

| Block | Min | Content |
|---|---|---|
| W | 5 | Warm-up and tension scan |
| T | 10-12 | Long tones (rotate registers); from Module 2B add octave slurs `.P` <-> `P` ([EX-TON](../exercises/EX-TON-tone-and-breath-drills.md)) |
| K | 15 | The module's new skill (below) |
| M | 12 | Melodies and alankars **with a tabla loop** once L15 is started |
| E | 10 | Ear (EX-EAR L2-L4), rhythm (EX-RHY), notation (EX-NOT), theory (EX-THY) in rotation |
| R | 3-5 | Record, rubric, log |

Every 7th practice day is a lighter review day (30-35 min): long tones, one item from each old
module, listening, weekly reflection.

## Modules

### Module 2A - Articulation (Weeks 7-8, in parallel with 2B)

- **Lesson:** [L13](../lessons/L13-articulation-tonguing-and-slurs.md). Objectives: FNG-11, FNG-16.
- **Practise:** 5 min of K each day: repeated notes with breath attacks, then light tonguing,
  on `S`, `R`, `G`, `.N`. Compare with slurred versions.
- **Gate:** `S S R R G G` at 80 BPM with each note separated and the tone unchanged (FNG-11).

### Module 2B - Tivra Ma and madhya Pa Dha Ni (Weeks 7-10)

- **Lesson:** [L14](../lessons/L14-tivra-ma-and-madhya-pa-dha-ni.md). Objectives: FNG-04, FNG-05, TON-07, INS-07.
- **Watch:** [R-V04](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#31-structured-beginner-series), [R-V13](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#32-single-technique-demonstrations) (octaves).
- **Week 7:** tivra `M` long tones; `G M G R S`. Octave slurs `.P -> P` (same fingers, faster
  narrower air) 5 x 2 min.
- **Week 8:** `P`, then `D` (`XXX XXO`, register 2), then `N`.
- **Week 9:** `S'` (register 2). Full Kalyan-scale `S R G M P D N S'` slowly (50 BPM).
- **Week 10:** alankars over the full middle octave ([EX-ALK](../exercises/EX-ALK-alankar-book.md) Version C) at 60 BPM.
- **Gate:** `.P` to `P` and back 5 times cleanly; full scale up and down at 60 BPM.

### Module 2C - Tala basics (Weeks 8-11)

- **Lesson:** [L15](../lessons/L15-taal-basics.md). Objectives: RHY-05, RHY-06, RHY-07, RHY-14, ENS-01.
- **Practise:** E-block 5 min daily: recite and clap keherwa, then dadra, then teentaal
  (with waves on khali). From Week 9, play all melodies over a keherwa or teentaal loop
  ([R-A04](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#71-drone-tanpura--shruti-box), [R-A05](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#71-drone-tanpura--shruti-box), or any tabla app).
- **Gate:** recite teentaal with claps/waves for 4 cycles; count 2 cycles silently and land on
  sam.

### Module 2D - Shuddha Ma and half-holing (Weeks 10-13)

- **Lesson:** [L16](../lessons/L16-shuddha-ma-and-half-holing.md). Objectives: FNG-07, PIT-08, THY-04.
- **Watch:** [R-V05](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#31-structured-beginner-series), [R-V14](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#32-single-technique-demonstrations).
- **Practise:** half-hole slide drill 5 min with a tuner; `G m G`, `m P m G`; `m <-> M` switching.
  Then Bilawal (major) scale and alankars. Reward melodies: Twinkle, Ode to Joy ([EX-MEL](../exercises/EX-MEL-practice-melodies.md)).
- **Gate:** `G m P m G` with `m` within +/-20 cents, 5 clean repetitions.

### Milestone M3 (about Week 13)

[M3 - Middle octave](../projects/M3-middle-octave.md): both Ma, full middle octave, alankars in
tala, two melodies. Strongly recommended: a teacher check before or after M3.

### Module 2E - Intonation with the drone (Weeks 13-15)

- **Lesson:** [L17](../lessons/L17-intonation-and-drone.md). Objectives: PIT-03, PIT-04, INS-09, TON-08, BRE-05.
- **Practise:** T-block becomes drone-tuning: each note held until it "locks" with the drone;
  bend 20 cents flat/sharp and return; swells (soft-loud-soft) with steady pitch.
- **Gate:** deliberately bend Sa 20 cents both ways and return to centre; 8-s swell on Sa
  within +/-15 cents.

### Module 2F - Song-learning system, first pass (Weeks 15-18)

- **Lesson:** [L18](../lessons/L18-song-learning-system.md). Objectives: SNG-01 (introduction), SNG-03, SNG-06, THY-14, BRE-04.
- **Practise:** apply the 12-step method to 2 simple melodies: one traditional bhajan or
  nursery melody you already know by voice, and one melody you have never seen notated.
  Use slow-down tools ([R-A14](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#72-tuner-metronome-recording-slow-down), [R-A17](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#72-tuner-metronome-recording-slow-down)).
- Start the **repertoire list** (SNG-08) in the progress tracker.

### Ear and theory track (all of Phase 2)

| Weeks | Ear ([EX-EAR](../exercises/EX-EAR-ear-training-system.md)) | Theory/notation |
|---|---|---|
| 7-10 | Level 2 (S vs P against drone), then Level 3 (S R G P) | [EX-NOT](../exercises/EX-NOT-notation-drills.md) rhythmic sargam; EX-RHY tala drills |
| 11-14 | Level 3 to pass; start Level 4 (all shuddha swaras) | 12 notes, intervals in semitones ([EX-THY](../exercises/EX-THY-theory-quizzes.md) Phase 2) |
| 15-18 | Level 4; phrase memory (EAR-09) with your old recordings | Song structure (THY-14); write out the notation of the songs from 2F |

- [ ] EX-EAR Level 3 passed, Level 4 started
- [ ] At least 6 pieces in your repertoire list, 3 of them played with a tabla loop
- [ ] Rubric: no category below 2 on the M3 recording, Tone and Pitch at least 3
