# Phase 5 - Advanced Musicianship and Independence (about month 20 onward)

[Mastery Map](../00-MASTERY-MAP.md) | Previous: [Phase 4](phase-4-intermediate-artistry.md)

**Duration:** open-ended. Advanced mastery takes many years even for full-time students. This
phase defines what to work on and how to know you are progressing. It does not promise an end
date.
**Goal:** full raga presentations, the hardest ragas, vilambit laya, the 3rd register,
live collaboration, original work, and the ability to design your own learning.
**Lesson:** [L37](../lessons/L37-full-raga-presentation-and-independence.md) (plus revisiting L31-L36 at greater depth).
**Milestone:** [M10 - Independent musician](../projects/M10-independent-musician.md).

## Practice structure

At this level, 45-60 min/day is enough to keep improving, but it no longer covers everything
each day. Use **themed weeks** (Recommendation):

| Week of month | Theme | Example content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Raga depth | Alap and vilambit in the month's main raga |
| 2 | Rhythm and speed | Taans, layakari, tihais, gamak taans |
| 3 | Repertoire and collaboration | Songs, accompaniment, ensemble rehearsal |
| 4 | Creation and review | Composition, recording project, monthly self-assessment |

Daily constants (about 15 min): warm-up, long tones in three octaves, one ear-training item.

## Ongoing assessment

- Monthly: one full recording, scored on the rubric, compared with last month's.
- Quarterly: a performance (live or recorded for an audience).
- Twice a year: a teacher assessment (Strongly recommended).
- Yearly: re-read [01-KNOWLEDGE-TREE.md](../01-KNOWLEDGE-TREE.md) and mark weak domains for the next year.

## What "advanced" looks like (Recommendation)

You can, without help:

- present a raga for 20+ minutes with a clear structure, correct grammar and stable intonation
- learn a new song by ear in a few sessions and write accurate notation
- accompany a singer in an unfamiliar song after hearing it once or twice
- diagnose a technical problem and design the practice to fix it
- explain your interpretive choices
