# Glossary

Plain-English definitions of every music term used in this curriculum, in A-Z order. The
"Taught in" column says where the term is explained properly. Symbols are in
[Conventions](05-CONVENTIONS-AND-NOTATION-KEY.md).

Many Indian terms have several spellings in English (for example taal/tala/tal,
meend/mind, bandish/bandeesh). This glossary uses the most common spelling and lists
alternatives in brackets.

## A

| Term | Meaning | Taught in |
|---|---|---|
| **Alankar** (alankaar, palta) | A practice pattern of notes repeated step by step up and down the scale (for example `SRG RGm Gm P...`). Also used more broadly to mean "ornament". | [L12](lessons/L12-alankars.md) |
| **Alap** (aalap) | The slow, unmetered (no tala) opening of a raga performance, where the raga is revealed note by note. | [L31](lessons/L31-alap-and-swar-vistar.md) |
| **Andolan** | A slow, gentle oscillation (swinging) of a note, used on particular notes in particular ragas. Written `G^` here. | [L33](lessons/L33-gamak-andolan-advanced-ornaments.md) |
| **Antara** | The second main section of a composition or song, usually exploring higher notes. Comes after the sthayi/mukhda. | [L18](lessons/L18-song-learning-system.md), [L32](lessons/L32-bandish-taans-layakari-tihai.md) |
| **Arti / aarti** | A devotional song sung while waving a lamp before a deity, for example "Om Jai Jagdish Hare". | [09-SONG-SUGGESTIONS](09-SONG-SUGGESTIONS.md) |
| **Aroh** (aaroh, arohana) | The ascending (upward) note order of a raga. | [L19](lessons/L19-what-is-a-raga.md) |
| **Audav** (odav) | A raga with 5 notes. See **jati**. | [L19](lessons/L19-what-is-a-raga.md) |
| **Avagraha** | In Bhatkhande notation, the symbol `ऽ` meaning "hold the previous note for this beat". Written `-` here. | [L27](lessons/L27-bhatkhande-notation.md) |
| **Avartan** (avart, avartana) | One complete cycle of a tala (for example 16 beats in teentaal). | [L15](lessons/L15-taal-basics.md) |
| **Avroh** (avaroh, avarohana) | The descending (downward) note order of a raga. | [L19](lessons/L19-what-is-a-raga.md) |
