# EX-IMP: Improvisation and Composition Prompts

[Back to Mastery Map](../00-MASTERY-MAP.md) | Lessons: [L29 Improvisation](../lessons/L29-improvisation-foundations.md), [L30 Composition](../lessons/L30-composition-foundations.md), [L31 Alap](../lessons/L31-alap-and-swar-vistar.md), [L32 Taans and tihai](../lessons/L32-bandish-taans-layakari-tihai.md)

**Objectives:** IMP-01 to IMP-07, CMP-01 to CMP-05, RHY-11, NOT-02

Improvising is not "making things up from nothing". It is **choosing freely within limits**.
Every prompt below gives you limits. Smaller limits make it easier to start.

**Always:** drone on, record yourself, and keep the best ideas in your notation book (NOT-10).
There are no "wrong" answers to these prompts, only ones that do or do not meet the stated
rules. Check with the self-assessment checklist at the end.

---

## Level 1: Call and response (from L09 onward; IMP-01)

| # | Prompt |
|---|---|
| 1.1 | Record yourself playing 8 two-bar "questions" using `S R G` only (leave 2 bars of silence after each). Play back and answer each question with your own 2 bars using the same notes. Rule: your answer ends on `S` |
| 1.2 | Same as 1.1, but your answer must copy the **rhythm** of the question with different notes |
| 1.3 | Same as 1.1, but your answer must copy the **notes** of the question with a different rhythm |
| 1.4 | After L11: questions and answers using `.P .D .N S R G`; answers end on `S` or `.D` |

## Level 2: Few notes over a drone (from L11/L14; IMP-02)

| # | Prompt |
|---|---|
| 2.1 | **3 notes:** improvise for 1 minute using only `S R G`, at a steady 70 BPM pulse (metronome). Long and short notes allowed. End on `S` |
| 2.2 | **Low 4:** `.D .N S R` for 1 minute, no metronome, slow, with long notes. Feel which notes want to "go home" to Sa |
| 2.3 | **Pentatonic (after L14):** `S R G P D` for 2 minutes. Rule: every phrase ends on `S`, `G` or `P` |
| 2.4 | **Rest rule:** same as 2.3, but leave at least 1 beat of silence between phrases. Silence is part of music |
| 2.5 | **One-note solo:** play only `G`, varying rhythm, length and loudness for 30 seconds. It is harder than it sounds and trains rhythmic imagination |

## Level 3: Variations on a melody (from L14/L16; IMP-03)

Take a melody from [EX-MEL](EX-MEL-practice-melodies.md) (for example 3.2 River Song or 4.1 Twinkle).

| # | Variation type | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Rhythm | Keep the notes, change the rhythm (for example pairs in one beat, dotted rhythms) |
| 3.2 | Fill | Fill long notes with neighbour notes (`G - -` becomes `G RG G`) |
| 3.3 | Octave | Play one phrase an octave lower or higher (if your range allows) |
| 3.4 | Ornament (after L23-L24) | Add one meend and two kans, no more |
| 3.5 | Mood | Play it sad (slow, soft, with meend) and then joyful (faster, tongued). Record both |

**Deliverable (IMP-03 test):** 4 distinct, recorded variations of one melody.

## Level 4: Raga-based improvisation (from L20-L21; IMP-04)

Use a raga you have learned. Check every phrase against its [raga card](../ragas/README.md).

| # | Prompt |
|---|---|
| 4.1 | **One-note-at-a-time vistar:** start on Sa. Each new phrase may add only **one** new note above or below the notes already used. Reach taar Sa in about 5 minutes (traditional alap logic, L31) |
| 4.2 | **Pakad anchors:** improvise 4 phrases, then play the pakad, repeat 3 times |
| 4.3 | **Nyas practice:** in Bhupali, end phrases alternately on Ga and Dha; in Yaman, on Ga and Ni |
| 4.4 | **Forbidden note alarm:** play Yaman for 2 minutes. Listen back: any shuddha `m` or any phrase starting `S R G` going up counts as a fault. Aim for zero |

## Level 5: Rhythmic improvisation (from L32; IMP-05, RHY-11)

| # | Prompt |
|---|---|
| 5.1 | Compose 3 tihais in Teentaal with the formula in [EX-RHY Level 9](EX-RHY-rhythm-drills.md#level-9-tihai-rhy-11-phase-4). Write them, then play them with a tabla loop, landing on sam |
| 5.2 | **Taan from an alankar:** take a Version C or C-penta alankar ([EX-ALK](EX-ALK-alankar-book.md)), play it in dugun inside the raga, and return to the composition's mukhda on time |
| 5.3 | **Improvised tihai:** in the last cycle before sam, improvise a short phrase and play it 3 times to land on sam. Start with phrase length 3 and no gap (L = 9, so start on beat 18 - 9 = 9) |

## Level 6: Song context (from L34; IMP-06)

| # | Prompt |
|---|---|
| 6.1 | Choose a song you know. Play the melody, and in each gap between vocal lines add a 2-beat fill using notes of the song's scale |
| 6.2 | Write a 4-bar intro and a 4-bar interlude for the same song |
| 6.3 | **Do-not-overplay rule:** record yourself accompanying a vocal recording. Count the moments your fills overlapped the singer's words. Target: zero |

## Composition prompts

### C1: First 16 beats (from L10/L14; CMP-01)

Write a 16-beat melody in Keherwa (2 lines of 8 beats) using `S R G P D`. Rules: it starts on
`S` or `G`, ends on `S`, and uses at least one held note (`-`). Play it from your notation and
record it.

### C2: Question and answer (from L16; CMP-02)

Write 4 lines of 8 beats:

| Line | Role | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Question | Ends on a note that is not `S` (for example `P` or `R`) |
| 2 | Answer | Same start as line 1, ends on `S` |
| 3 | Contrast | Higher (or lower) register than lines 1-2 |
| 4 | Answer | Return to line 2 (exactly or slightly varied) |

Label each line. This "A A' B A'" shape is used in countless songs.

### C3: Raga bandish sthayi (from L32; CMP-03)

Compose a 16-beat sthayi in Bhupali or Yaman in Teentaal. Rules: obey the raga card; place an
important note (vadi) on sam; the mukhda (first few notes) should be easy to return to after
improvisation. Later, add an antara that rises to the taar saptak.

### C4: Melody for words (from L30; CMP-04)

Take a short poem or 4 lines of a prayer you know (or write your own), clap the natural rhythm
of the words, and set it to a melody in Keherwa or Dadra. Rule: stressed syllables fall on
strong beats. Write the syllables under the notes.

### C5: Complete piece (Phase 5; CMP-05)

A 3-5 minute original piece: slow introduction (alap-like), a composed melody in a tala,
2 variations, and an ending with a tihai. Notate the composed parts; record the whole piece
(Milestone M10).

## Self-assessment checklist (score each 1-4)

| Aspect | Question |
|---|---|
| Rules | Did I keep the prompt's rules (notes, endings, raga)? |
| Pulse | Where there was a beat, did I stay on it? |
| Shape | Do my phrases have a beginning, a peak and an end, or do they wander? |
| Repetition and contrast | Did I repeat good ideas (with small changes) instead of always new ones? |
| Space | Did I leave silences? |
| Endings | Do phrases resolve convincingly (usually to Sa or a resting note)? |
| Listening | On playback, would I want to hear this again? |

Write one sentence: "Next time I will...". Improvement comes from that sentence.
