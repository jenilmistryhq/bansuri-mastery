# EX-MEL: Graded Practice Melodies

[Back to Mastery Map](../00-MASTERY-MAP.md) | Notation: [Conventions](../05-CONVENTIONS-AND-NOTATION-KEY.md) | Reading: [L10](../lessons/L10-reading-and-writing-sargam.md)

**Objectives:** SNG-02, NOT-01, NOT-03, RHY-03, RHY-06, BRE-04

## Rules for every melody

- Learn only melodies whose **level is unlocked** (see each heading).
- Process: (1) clap the rhythm while saying the sargam, (2) sing it with the drone, (3) play it
  slowly (60 BPM), (4) play it at the target tempo, (5) play it from memory, (6) record it and
  score it with the rubric in [L08](../lessons/L08-recording-and-self-assessment.md).
- `,` = breathe here. If there is no breath mark, breathe at a line end.
- A melody is **in repertoire** when you can play it from memory, in time, 3 times in a row
  without stopping. Add it to your repertoire list ([SNG-08](../knowledge-tree/D13-SNG-songs.md)).

**Sources:** melodies marked **Traditional** are public-domain traditional or nursery tunes.
Melodies marked **Original** were composed for this curriculum. There are deliberately **no
copyrighted film songs here**; you will learn those by ear with [L18](../lessons/L18-song-learning-system.md).

**Extra rhythm mark used below:** `-R` inside one beat means "keep the previous note for the
first half of the beat, then play R on the second half" (a dotted rhythm).

## Level 1: Sa Re Ga (unlocked by [L09](../lessons/L09-fingering-sa-re-ga.md))

### 1.1 Morning Steps (Original, Keherwa 8 beats per line, 60-80 BPM)

```
S R G - | G R S - ||
S S R R | G - - - ||
G R S R | G - R - ||
S R G R | S - - - ||
```

### 1.2 Hot Cross Buns (Traditional, 4 beats per bar, 60-90 BPM)

Verified against: Wikipedia, "Hot Cross Buns (song)", score (the familiar "Alternative" tune), https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_Cross_Buns_(song).

```
G R S - | G R S - | SS SS RR RR | G R S - ||
```

### 1.3 Three-Note Waltz (Original, Dadra 6 beats per line)

```
S - R | G - - ||
G - R | S - - ||
S R G | R S - ||
G R S | S - - ||
```

### 1.4 Call and Answer (Original, Keherwa 8 beats per line, 60-80 BPM)

```
S R G R | S - - - ||
G G R R | S - S - ||
S R G G | R - R - ||
G R S R | S - - - ||
```

Lines 1 and 3 end "open"; lines 2 and 4 answer and come home to Sa. Separate the repeated notes
(`G G`, `R R`) with a light "hu".

## Level 3: Full madhya with tivra Ma (unlocked by [L14](../lessons/L14-tivra-ma-and-madhya-pa-dha-ni.md))

### 3.1 Mary Had a Little Lamb (Traditional, 4 beats per bar)

Verified against: "Mary had a little lamb" (ABC, John Chambers' demo tunes), https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/abc/demo/Tunes/MaryHadALittleLamb/0000; and "Lesson 3 - Mary Had a Little Lamb" (ABC, Cecilio violin first lesson), https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=gitlab.com/chrisspen/abc-music/abc-music-master.zip/abc-music-master/cecilio-violin-first-lesson/03-2-mary-had-a-little-lamb/0000.

```
G R S R | G G G - | R R R - | G P P - |
G R S R | G G G G | R R G R | S - - - ||
```

Note the jump `G P` (register 1 to register 2).

### 3.2 River Song (Original, Keherwa; Bhupali notes)

```
S R G P  | D - P G  ||
R G R S  | .D - S - ||
G P D S' | D P G -  ||
P G R G  | S - - -  ||
```

### 3.3 Lamp of Yaman (Original, Teentaal 16 beats per line; Kalyan notes)

```
.N R G - | G M P - | M G R - | .N R S - , ||
G M D N  | S' - N D | P M G R | .N R S - ||
```

### 3.4 Little Dadra (Original, Dadra with 2 notes per beat)

```
SR G P  | D P G ||
GR S .D | S - - ||
```

### 3.5 Amazing Grace, first half (Traditional, 3 beats per bar, 60-80 BPM)

The hymn tune, usually called "New Britain", is traditional and in the public domain. It starts
with a one-beat pickup on `.P`. `GS` = two notes in one beat.

Verified against: Colin Hume, "Amazing Grace" (ABC), https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=colinhume.com/ABC.txt/0030; and Paul Hardy's Session Tunebook, "Amazing Grace / New Britain" (ABC), https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=pghardy.net/tunebooks/pgh_session_tunebook/0009.

```
.P | S - GS | G - R | S - .D | .P - .P |
S - GS | G - R | P - - | P - - ||
```

### 3.6 Oh! Susanna, verse (Stephen Foster, 1848, public domain, 4 beats per bar, 70-90 BPM)

Two notes per beat almost everywhere, starting with a one-beat pickup `SR`. The rhythm is
simplified: the original has a few dotted (long-short) pairs, which you can copy by ear later.
The chorus needs shuddha Ma, so it is not included here.

Verified against: Paul Hardy's Session Tunebook, "Oh Susanna" (ABC), https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=pghardy.net/tunebooks/pgh_session_tunebook/0446; and the Digital Tradition, "Oh Susanna!" (ABC), https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=sniff.numachi.com/~rickheit/dtrad/abc_dtrad.tar.gz/abc_dtrad/OSUSANNA/0000.

```
SR | GP PD PG SR | GG RS R SR |
GP PD PG SR | GG RR S - ||
```

### 3.7 Auld Lang Syne (Traditional Scottish, public domain, 4 beats per bar, 60-80 BPM)

Verse (lines 1-2) and chorus (lines 3-4), with a one-beat pickup on `.P`. Uses only
`.P .D S R G P D S'`, no Ma at all. `-S`, `-G` and `-.D` are the dotted long-short pairs (see
the extra rhythm mark at the top of this page): the "Should auld" lilt. Some editions start the last chorus phrase
on `D` instead of `S'` (end of line 3).

Verified against: Wikipedia, "Auld Lang Syne", score, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auld_Lang_Syne; Paul Hardy's Songs Tunebook, "Auld Lang Syne" (ABC), https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=pghardy.net/tunebooks/pgh_songs_tunebook/0001; and "Auld Lang Syne" for violin (ABC), https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=gitlab.com/chrisspen/abc-music/abc-music-master.zip/abc-music-master/violin/auld-lang-syne-g/0000.

```
.P | S -S S G | R -S R G | S -S G P | D - - D |
P -G G S | R -S R G | S -.D .D .P | S - - D |
P -G G S | R -S R D | P -G G P | D - - S' |
P -G G S | R -S R G | S -.D .D .P | S - - ||
```

## Level 4: Shuddha Ma (unlocked by [L16](../lessons/L16-shuddha-ma-and-half-holing.md))

**Check `m` with a tuner (F) before each piece.** Many famous Western tunes need shuddha Ma,
which is why they come after L16 on a bansuri.

### 4.1 Twinkle Twinkle Little Star (Traditional)

Verified against: Wikipedia, "Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star", score, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twinkle,_Twinkle,_Little_Star; and Frank Nordberg, "Ah! vous dirai-je, maman" (ABC), https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/abc/demo/Tunes/AhVousDiraiJeMaman/0000.

```
S S P P | D D P - | m m G G | R R S - |
P P m m | G G R - | P P m m | G G R - |
S S P P | D D P - | m m G G | R R S - ||
```

### 4.2 Ode to Joy (Beethoven, public domain)

Verified against: Wikipedia, "Ode to Joy", score, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ode_to_Joy; and "Ode To Joy" (ABC, John Chambers' demo tunes), https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/abc/demo/Tunes/OdeToJoy/0000.

```
G G m P | P m G R | S S R G | G -R R - ||
G G m P | P m G R | S S R G | R -S S - ||
```

`G -R` = Ga for one and a half beats, Re on the last half beat.

### 4.3 Jingle Bells, chorus (James Lord Pierpont, public domain)

Verified against: Paul Hardy's Xmas Tunebook, "Jingle Bells" (ABC), https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=pghardy.net/tunebooks/pgh_xmas_tunebook/0034; and Colin Hume, "Jingle Bells" (ABC), https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=colinhume.com/ABC.txt/0692.

```
G G G - | G G G - | G P S R | G - - - |
m m m m | m G G GG | G R R G | R - P - ||
```

### 4.4 Happy Birthday (traditional melody, public domain)

Verified against: "Good Morning and Birthday Song", The Everyday Song Book (1927), ABC transcription by John Chambers, https://trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/book/EverydaySongBook/016_Good_Morning_and_Birthday_Song.abc; and Wikipedia, "Happy Birthday to You", score, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Happy_Birthday_to_You.

**Full melody, notes only** (Sa = C, starting low on mandra Pa):

```
.P .P .D .P S .N
.P .P .D .P R S
.P .P P G S .N .D
m m G S R S
```

**Unlocking:** lines 1 and 2 use only `.P .D .N S R`, so you can play them **after
[L11](../lessons/L11-lower-notes-ni-dha-pa.md)**. Line 3 needs madhya `P` (L14) and line 4 needs
shuddha `m` (L16), so the **complete song unlocks after
[L16](../lessons/L16-shuddha-ma-and-half-holing.md)**.

**Rhythm (approximate: learn the real rhythm by ear).** Everyone knows how this song goes, so
it is the perfect first exercise in fitting notes to a rhythm you already have in your head.
Sing it with sargam syllables first, then play. The written rhythm below is a simplified guide,
counted in 3 beats per bar, with a two-note pickup:

```
.P.P | .D .P S  | .N - .P.P | .D .P R | S - .P.P |
       P  G  S  | .N .D mm  | G  S  R | S - -    ||
```

The real song lengthens some notes (especially on the name, "dear ..."). Trust your ear and a
recording over this guide.

G m G R  | S - - - , ||

### 4.6 Frere Jacques (Traditional French round, public domain, 4 beats per bar, 60-90 BPM)

Verified against: "Frere Jacques", Singing Together (BBC, 1951), via folkinfo.org (ABC), https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/abc/mirror/folkinfo.org/Frere_Jacques/0000; and the Digital Tradition, "Frere Jacques" (ABC), https://abcnotation.com/tunePage?a=sniff.numachi.com/~rickheit/dtrad/abc_dtrad.tar.gz/abc_dtrad/FRERJACQ/0000.

```
S R G S | S R G S | G m P - | G m P - |
PD Pm G S | PD Pm G S | S .P S - | S .P S - ||
```

It is a round: record yourself playing it, then play it again starting when the recording
reaches bar 3. The two parts should fit together.

### 4.7 London Bridge (Traditional, public domain, 4 beats per bar, 70-90 BPM)

Verified against: Wikipedia, "London Bridge Is Falling Down", score, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Bridge_Is_Falling_Down; and flutetunes.com, "London Bridge is Falling Down" (MIDI), https://www.flutetunes.com/tunes.php?id=126.

The first beat of each line is really a long-short (dotted) pair; play it as two even notes
first, then copy the lilt by ear.

```
PD Pm Gm P | RG m Gm P |
PD Pm Gm P | R P G S ||
```

## Level 5: Into taar saptak (unlocked by [L22](../lessons/L22-taar-saptak.md))

### 5.1 Hilltop (Original, Keherwa)

```
S G P S' | R' S' N D  ||
P - S' - | G' - R' S' ||
N D P G  | R - S -    ||
```

### 5.2 Mountain Echo (Original, Keherwa 8 beats per line, 60-76 BPM)

```
P D N S' | R' - S' - ||
G' R' S' N | D - P - ||
P D S' R' | G' - R' S' ||
N D P m | G R S - ||
```

The top note is `G'`, your comfortable ceiling. Keep the air focused rather than loud on
`R'` and `G'`, and let the last line relax back down to Sa.

Also play **4.1 Twinkle** one octave higher (start on `S'`, the `P` notes become `P'`, which is
register 3) only if your flute's `P'` is reliable. Otherwise skip that.

## Level 6: Komal notes and meend (unlocked by [L25](../lessons/L25-komal-swaras-and-12-notes.md); 6.2 also needs [L23](../lessons/L23-meend.md))

### 6.1 Rain Song (Original, Keherwa; Kafi notes)

```
S R g m  | P - m g  ||
R g R S  | .n .D S - , ||
m P D n  | S' - n D ||
P m g R  | S - - -  ||
```

### 6.2 Longing (Original, Dadra, slow 50 BPM; Shivranjani notes with meend)

```
S R g    | g~R - S ||
.D S R   | g - -   ||
P D P    | g~R S - ||
R g R    | S - -   ||
```

`g~R` = glide from komal Ga down to Re inside one beat.

### 6.3 Dusk Waltz (Original, Dadra 6 beats per line, 60-72 BPM; Kafi notes)

```
S R g | R - S ||
.n .D .n | S - - ||
m P n | P - m ||
g R g | S - - ||
```

Check `g`, `n` and `.n` against the drone before you start: half-holes drift sharp when the
finger slides off too far ([L25](../lessons/L25-komal-swaras-and-12-notes.md)).

## By-ear projects (no notation given, on purpose)

These traditional devotional melodies are widely known. Learn them **by ear** with the 12-step
process in [L18](../lessons/L18-song-learning-system.md) once you reach Phase 3. Find a recording
you like, find its Sa, check its range fits your flute, and write your own notation.

- "Raghupati Raghav Raja Ram" (traditional bhajan)
- "Om Jai Jagdish Hare" (traditional aarti)

More bhajans, patriotic songs and film songs, staged by difficulty and checked against your
flute's range: [09-SONG-SUGGESTIONS.md](../09-SONG-SUGGESTIONS.md).

**Film songs:** choose your own. Before you start, do the **range check** in L18: note the
lowest and highest notes. With your C flute (Sa = C) the comfortable range is about `.P` (G4) to
`G'` (E6). If a song does not fit at its original pitch, L26 shows how to move Sa or choose
another flute.
