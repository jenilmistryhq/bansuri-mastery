# EX-MEL: Graded Practice Melodies

[Back to Mastery Map](../00-MASTERY-MAP.md) | Notation: [Conventions](../05-CONVENTIONS-AND-NOTATION-KEY.md) | Reading: [L10](../lessons/L10-reading-and-writing-sargam.md)

**Objectives:** SNG-02, NOT-01, NOT-03, RHY-03, RHY-06, BRE-04

## Rules for every melody

- Learn only melodies whose **level is unlocked** (see each heading).
- Process: (1) clap the rhythm while saying the sargam, (2) sing it with the drone, (3) play it
  slowly (60 BPM), (4) play it at the target tempo, (5) play it from memory, (6) record it and
  score it with the rubric in [L08](../lessons/L08-recording-and-self-assessment.md).
- `,` = breathe here. If there is no breath mark, breathe at a line end.
- A melody is **in repertoire** when you can play it from memory, in time, 3 times in a row
  without stopping. Add it to your repertoire list ([SNG-08](../knowledge-tree/D13-SNG-songs.md)).

**Sources:** melodies marked **Traditional** are public-domain traditional or nursery tunes.
Melodies marked **Original** were composed for this curriculum. There are deliberately **no
copyrighted film songs here**; you will learn those by ear with [L18](../lessons/L18-song-learning-system.md).

**Extra rhythm mark used below:** `-R` inside one beat means "keep the previous note for the
first half of the beat, then play R on the second half" (a dotted rhythm).

## Level 1: Sa Re Ga (unlocked by [L09](../lessons/L09-fingering-sa-re-ga.md))

### 1.1 Morning Steps (Original, Keherwa 8 beats per line, 60-80 BPM)

```
S R G - | G R S - ||
S S R R | G - - - ||
G R S R | G - R - ||
S R G R | S - - - ||
```

### 1.2 Hot Cross Buns (Traditional, 4 beats per bar, 60-90 BPM)

```
G R S - | G R S - | SS SS RR RR | G R S - ||
```

### 1.3 Three-Note Waltz (Original, Dadra 6 beats per line)

```
S - R | G - - ||
G - R | S - - ||
S R G | R S - ||
G R S | S - - ||
```

## Level 4: Shuddha Ma (unlocked by [L16](../lessons/L16-shuddha-ma-and-half-holing.md))

**Check `m` with a tuner (F) before each piece.** Many famous Western tunes need shuddha Ma,
which is why they come after L16 on a bansuri.

### 4.1 Twinkle Twinkle Little Star (Traditional)

```
S S P P | D D P - | m m G G | R R S - |
P P m m | G G R - | P P m m | G G R - |
S S P P | D D P - | m m G G | R R S - ||
```

### 4.2 Ode to Joy (Beethoven, public domain)

```
G G m P | P m G R | S S R G | G -R R - ||
G G m P | P m G R | S S R G | R -S S - ||
```

`G -R` = Ga for one and a half beats, Re on the last half beat.

### 4.3 Jingle Bells, chorus (James Lord Pierpont, public domain)

```
G G G - | G G G - | G P S R | G - - - |
m m m m | m G G GG | G R R G | R - P - ||
```

### 4.4 Happy Birthday (traditional melody, public domain)

**Full melody, notes only** (Sa = C, starting low on mandra Pa):

```
Line 1:  .P .P .D .P S .N
Line 2:  .P .P .D .P R S
Line 3:  .P .P P G S .N .D
Line 4:  m m G S R S
```

**Unlocking:** lines 1 and 2 use only `.P .D .N S R`, so you can play them **after
[L11](../lessons/L11-lower-notes-ni-dha-pa.md)**. Line 3 needs madhya `P` (L14) and line 4 needs
shuddha `m` (L16), so the **complete song unlocks after
[L16](../lessons/L16-shuddha-ma-and-half-holing.md)**.

**Rhythm (approximate: learn the real rhythm by ear).** Everyone knows how this song goes, so
it is the perfect first exercise in fitting notes to a rhythm you already have in your head.
Sing it with sargam syllables first, then play. The written rhythm below is a simplified guide,
counted in 3 beats per bar, with a two-note pickup:

```
.P.P | .D .P S  | .N - .P.P | .D .P R | S - .P.P |
       P  G  S  | .N .D mm  | G  S  R | S - -    ||
```

The real song lengthens some notes (especially on the name, "dear ..."). Trust your ear and a
recording over this guide.

```
N D P G  | R - S -    ||
```

register 3) only if your flute's `P'` is reliable. Otherwise skip that.

## Level 6: Komal notes and meend (unlocked by [L25](../lessons/L25-komal-swaras-and-12-notes.md); 6.2 also needs [L23](../lessons/L23-meend.md))

### 6.1 Rain Song (Original, Keherwa; Kafi notes)

```
S R g m  | P - m g  ||
R g R S  | .n .D S - , ||
m P D n  | S' - n D ||
P m g R  | S - - -  ||
```

### 6.2 Longing (Original, Dadra, slow 50 BPM; Shivranjani notes with meend)

```
S R g    | g~R - S ||
.D S R   | g - -   ||
P D P    | g~R S - ||
R g R    | S - -   ||
```

`g~R` = glide from komal Ga down to Re inside one beat.

## By-ear projects (no notation given, on purpose)

These traditional devotional melodies are widely known. Learn them **by ear** with the 12-step
process in [L18](../lessons/L18-song-learning-system.md) once you reach Phase 3. Find a recording
you like, find its Sa, check its range fits your flute, and write your own notation.

- "Raghupati Raghav Raja Ram" (traditional bhajan)
- "Om Jai Jagdish Hare" (traditional aarti)

More bhajans, patriotic songs and film songs, staged by difficulty and checked against your
flute's range: [09-SONG-SUGGESTIONS.md](../09-SONG-SUGGESTIONS.md).

**Film songs:** choose your own. Before you start, do the **range check** in L18: note the
lowest and highest notes. With your C flute (Sa = C) the comfortable range is about `.P` (G4) to
`G'` (E6). If a song does not fit at its original pitch, L26 shows how to move Sa or choose
another flute.
