# EX-TON: Tone and Breath Drills

[Back to Mastery Map](../00-MASTERY-MAP.md) | Lessons: [L03 Breathing](../lessons/L03-breathing-basics.md), [L04 First sound](../lessons/L04-first-sound.md), [L07 Long tones](../lessons/L07-long-tones-tuner-and-drone.md), [L14 Registers](../lessons/L14-tivra-ma-and-madhya-pa-dha-ni.md), [L17 Intonation](../lessons/L17-intonation-and-drone.md)

**Objectives:** TON-03 to TON-10, BRE-01 to BRE-07, PIT-03, PIT-04, PIT-07

This is your daily **block B** (tone) material: about 8-12 minutes every day for the whole
course. Tone is never "finished": professional flautists still do long tones daily.

> **Safety (BRE-06):** if you feel dizzy or light-headed, stop, breathe normally through your
> nose, and sit for a minute. Dizziness means you are over-breathing, not that you are doing it
> "hard enough". Never push through it. See [TS-07](../troubleshooting/TS-07-breath-dizziness-and-endurance.md).

## Part 1: Breath drills (no flute; unlocked by L03)

| # | Drill | How | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Low breathing check | Lie on your back or sit tall, one hand on belly, one on chest. Breathe in through the nose; only the belly hand should rise | 10 calm breaths, chest still |
| B2 | Hiss exhale | Inhale low, then release air through the teeth on a steady "sssss". Time it | Week 1: 15 s; Week 3: 20 s; Week 6: 30 s, even volume all the way |
| B3 | Quick inhale | Metronome 60. Exhale "sss" for 7 beats, inhale through the mouth corners on beat 8, repeat 4 times | Silent, full breath in one beat, no shoulder lift |
| B4 | Breath ladder | Hiss for 8 beats at 60, rest 4; then 12; then 16 | Phase 2: all three without strain |
| B5 | Pulse "hu" | On one breath: "hu hu hu hu" (4 per beat at 60), from the belly, no throat clicks | Prepares breath attacks (FNG-16) and later breath gamak |

## Part 2: Long tones (unlocked by L07)

**Method:** drone on C. Breathe low. Start the note softly and cleanly, hold it at a steady
volume, end it cleanly by stopping the air (no "pew" sound). Watch the tuner for the first
seconds, then **close your eyes and listen** for the drone and your note "locking" without
wavering ([PIT-03](../knowledge-tree/D06-PIT-pitch.md)). Rest 1 breath between notes.

### Targets by week (Recommendation; adjust to your progress)

| Week / unlock | Notes | Hold | Tuner target | Order |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 (L04) | `S` | 4 s | not yet | 6 repetitions |
| Week 2 (L07, L09) | `S R G` | 6-8 s | +/-25 cents | S, R, G, R, S |
| Weeks 3-4 (L11) | add `.N .D .P` | 8 s (`.P` 6 s) | +/-20 cents | S down to `.P` and back |
| Weeks 5-6 | all of the above | 10-12 s | +/-15 cents | Two notes per day get extra attention: your weakest |
| After L14 | add `M P D N S'` | 8 s | +/-15 cents | Full madhya saptak |
| After L16 | add `m` | 8 s | +/-15 cents | |
| Phase 3 (BRE-07) | `S` and `.P` | 20 s | +/-15 cents | Endurance test |
| After L22 | `S' R' G'` | 6-8 s | +/-15 cents | Soft taar notes are the hardest skill here |
| After L25 | `r g d n` | 6 s | +/-20 cents | Half-hole intonation |

**Tuner graph (optional, very useful):** R-A10 Tunable shows pitch as a line over time. A good
long tone is a **flat straight line**; a sagging line means the air speed is dropping; a wavy
line means unsteady breath support.

## Part 3: Register and overblowing drills (unlocked by L14)

| # | Drill | Notes | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Octave slurs | `.P P .P P`, `.D D .D D`, `.N N .N N`, `S S' S S'` (same fingering; only air changes) | Clean jumps, no in-between squeak, 5/5 at 60 BPM |
| R2 | Register slur up | Hold `.P` 4 beats, then without stopping the air, speed it up until `P` sounds; hold 4 beats | Smooth "click" into the upper octave |
| R3 | Harmonics (Fact: faster air gives the 2nd and 3rd harmonics, an octave and a twelfth up, so G5 then D6 (Verified: UNSW Music Acoustics; HyperPhysics; Wikipedia: Altissimo). Uncertain: how easily your flute gives D6) | Finger `XXX XXX`. Blow gently (G4), faster (G5 = `P`), faster still: some flutes give D6 (taar `R'`) | Explore only; stop if you need to force. Teaches air speed, not force |
| R4 | Register crossings | `G P G P`, `M D M D`, `G' to G` slurs | Even tone across the change |
| R5 | Top-down long tones | Start on `S'`, hold, then `N`, `D`, `P`... down to `.P` | Tone stays the same "colour" from top to bottom (TON-10) |

**Principle:** the upper register needs **faster, narrower air** (smaller lip opening, air
aimed slightly higher), **not more force**. Blowing hard makes notes sharp and harsh.

## Part 4: Dynamics and pitch control (unlocked by L17)

| # | Drill | How | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Swell | On `S`: 4 beats getting louder, 4 beats getting softer (60 BPM) | Pitch within +/-15 cents throughout (TON-08) |
| D2 | Compensation | Louder notes on a flute tend to go **sharp**; softer ones **flat**. While swelling, roll the flute slightly **in** as you get louder and **out** as you get softer | Tuner needle stays still |
| D3 | Bends | On `S`, deliberately bend 20 cents flat (roll in) and 20 cents sharp (roll out), then return to centre | 3 controlled bends each way (PIT-04) |
| D4 | Echo | Play a 4-note phrase loud, then repeat it soft | Soft version still clear and in tune |

## Part 5: Clean starts and endings

| # | Drill | Target |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Breath starts: `S` started with a soft "hu" (no tongue), 8 times | Note speaks immediately, no "fff" before it |
| A2 | Tongue starts (after L13): "ta" or "da" on `S R G` | Crisp, not explosive |
| A3 | Endings: stop the note by stopping the air, not by closing the lips or dropping the flute | No pitch drop at the end |

## Tone-quality checklist

Use on a recording of long tones (1 = poor, 4 = excellent). Same scale as the rubric in
[L08](../lessons/L08-recording-and-self-assessment.md).

| Aspect | Question | Score 1-4 |
|---|---|---|
| Core | Is there a clear "centre" to the sound, or is it mostly air? | |
| Hiss | How much breath noise is audible? | |
| Stability | Does the note waver in pitch or volume? | |
| Pitch | Does it lock with the drone? | |
| Start | Does the note start cleanly and immediately? | |
| End | Does it end cleanly without a pitch drop? | |
| Evenness | Do different notes sound like the same instrument and player? | |

**Diagnosis:** an airy tone means checking embouchure and angle ([TS-01](../troubleshooting/TS-01-no-sound-or-airy-tone.md)).
Squeaks or the wrong octave: [TS-02](../troubleshooting/TS-02-squeaks-and-wrong-octave.md). Weak low notes:
[TS-03](../troubleshooting/TS-03-low-notes-wont-sound.md). Out of tune: [TS-05](../troubleshooting/TS-05-pitch-out-of-tune.md).
