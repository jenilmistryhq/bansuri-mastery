## Level 1: Reading sargam (from L10; NOT-01)

Play each line at 60 BPM, one beat per space-separated group. Only use lines whose notes you
have unlocked (S R G: L09; `.N .D .P`: L11; P D N M: L14; m: L16).

| # | Line | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | `S R G R | S - - -` | L09 |
| 1.2 | `G G R S | R - - -` | L09 |
| 1.3 | `S .N .D .N | S - R -` | L11 |
| 1.4 | `.P .D S R | G R S -` | L11 |
| 1.5 | `G P D P | G R S -` | L14 |
| 1.6 | `.N R G M | D N S' -` | L14 |
| 1.7 | `S' N D P | M G R S` | L14 |
| 1.8 | `G m P m | G R S -` | L16 |

**Octave check:** say aloud which saptak each note is in before playing. Answer for 1.6:
`.N` mandra; `R G M D N` madhya; `S'` taar.

## Level 2: Writing what you play (from L10; NOT-02)

1. Play a phrase you invent (4-8 notes). Write it down. Put the paper away.
2. The next day, play from your paper. Does it match what you remember? If not, find out which
   one is wrong by recording yourself next time **before** writing.
3. Write down 5 phrases from songs you can hum (sing first, find on the flute, write). Check by
   playing from your notation along with the original (L18 step 11).

No answer key: **the check is playing your notation back.**

## Level 4: Find the errors (from L16; NOT-05)

Each line has **one or more** mistakes compared with the correct melody given. Find them.

| # | Melody (correct version known to you) | Faulty notation |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Twinkle Twinkle, line 1-2 (see [EX-MEL](EX-MEL-practice-melodies.md)) | `S S P P D D P - | M M G G R R S -` |
| 4.2 | Mary Had a Little Lamb, line 1 | `G R S R G G G -` written as `G R S R G G -` |
| 4.3 | Bhupali aroh | `S R G m P D S'` |
| 4.4 | Yaman pakad | `S R G R, .N R S, P M G R, .N R S` |

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answer key</summary>

- **4.1** `M` (tivra) must be `m` (shuddha): `m m G G R R S -`. A very common online error is
  not saying which Ma.
- **4.2** One beat is missing: the third `G` should be held (`G G G -`) so the line has 8 beats.
  Missing beats are the most common rhythm error in online notations.
- **4.3** Bhupali has no Ma: `S R G P D S'`.
- **4.4** The pakad starts on mandra Ni, not Sa: `.N R G R, .N R S, P M G R, .N R S`.

</details>

## Level 5: Bhatkhande (Devanagari) to ASCII (from L27; NOT-04)

Bhatkhande marks (fonts may display them slightly differently):

| Mark | Meaning | ASCII |
|---|---|---|
| line **below** a note (रे॒, ग॒, ध॒, नि॒) | komal | lowercase `r g d n` |
| vertical line **above** Ma (म॑) | tivra Ma | `M` |
| dot **below** (ऩि, ध़) | mandra | `.N`, `.D` |
| dot **above** (सां, रें) | taar | `S'`, `R'` |
| ऽ (avagraha) | hold the previous note | `-` |
| arc under notes | those notes share one beat | written together, e.g. `GR` |
| X, 0, 2, 3 above beats | sam, khali, tali | same marks |

Convert to ASCII:

| # | Bhatkhande |
|---|---|
| 5.1 | सा रे ग प |
| 5.2 | ऩि रे ग म॑ |
| 5.3 | सां नि ध प |
| 5.4 | सा रे॒ ग म प ध॒ नि सां |
| 5.5 | ग ऽ रे सा |
| 5.6 | ध़ सा रे ग |

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answer key</summary>

5.1 `S R G P` | 5.2 `.N R G M` | 5.3 `S' N D P` | 5.4 `S r G m P d N S'` (Bhairav) |
5.5 `G - R S` | 5.6 `.D S R G`

</details>

## Level 6: Western letters and sargam (from L26/L28; THY-03, NOT-07)

**On your flute, Sa = C:** C=S, Db=r, D=R, Eb=g, E=G, F=m, F#=M, G=P, Ab=d, A=D, Bb=n, B=N.

Convert (Sa = C):

| # | Western | | # | Sargam |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6.1 | C D E G A | | 6.5 | `S G P S'` |
| 6.2 | E F# G A B | | 6.6 | `R m D` |
| 6.3 | G A Bb C | | 6.7 | `.N S R g` |
| 6.4 | A G F E D C | | 6.8 | `P M G R S` |

Now **Sa = G** (Pa of your flute as Sa): G=S, A=R, B=G, C=m, C#=M, D=P, E=D, F=n, F#=N.

| # | Western (Sa = G) | | # | Sargam (Sa = G) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6.9 | G A B C D | | 6.11 | `S R G P D` |
| 6.10 | D E F# G | | 6.12 | `S n D P` |

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answer key</summary>

6.1 `S R G P D` (Bhupali) | 6.2 `G M P D N` | 6.3 `P D n S'` | 6.4 `D P m G R S` |
6.5 C E G C (upper C) | 6.6 D F A | 6.7 B C D Eb (B in the lower octave) | 6.8 G F# E D C |
6.9 `S R G m P` | 6.10 `P D N S'` | 6.11 G A B D E | 6.12 G F E D

</details>

## Level 7: Treble-staff note names (from L28; NOT-06)

The **treble clef** staff has 5 lines. From bottom to top the **lines** are **E G B D F**, and
the **spaces** are **F A C E**. On your flute: mandra Pa (G4) sits on the **2nd line**; Sa (C5)
is in the **3rd space**; taar Sa (C6) is **two ledger lines above** the staff.

Name the note and its sargam (Sa = C):

| # | Position |
|---|---|
| 7.1 | 3rd space |
| 7.2 | 2nd line |
| 7.3 | 4th line |
| 7.4 | 4th space |
| 7.5 | top (5th) line |
| 7.6 | 2nd space |
| 7.7 | 3rd line |
| 7.8 | 1st line |

<details markdown="1">
<summary>Answer key</summary>

7.1 C5 = `S` | 7.2 G4 = `.P` | 7.3 D5 = `R` | 7.4 E5 = `G` | 7.5 F5 = `m` |
7.6 A4 = `.D` | 7.7 B4 = `.N` | 7.8 E4 = below your flute's range (mandra Ga is lower than `.P`;
it cannot be played on your flute)

</details>
