# Bansuri Mastery

A practical, research-based learning system for the Indian bamboo flute, taking you from
zero musical knowledge toward advanced, independent musicianship.

**Built for:** a 6-hole **C natural medium** bansuri (Sa = C5, about 523 Hz), 45-60 minutes a
day, with classical training mixed with bhajans and film songs. If your flute is different,
see [Conventions, section 7](05-CONVENTIONS-AND-NOTATION-KEY.md#7-if-your-flute-is-different).

## Start here

> **Prefer an app?** The [Bansuri Mastery app](https://jenilmistryhq.github.io/bansuri-mastery/) teaches this course one day at a time, with guided sessions, timers, the Practice Bench built in, milestone tests, a practice journal and progress tracking. It works offline and keeps your progress on your device.

1. Read this page (5 minutes).
2. Skim [00-MASTERY-MAP.md](00-MASTERY-MAP.md) to see the whole journey.
3. Do **Day 0**: [phases/phase-0-setup.md](phases/phase-0-setup.md).
4. From Day 1, follow [phases/phase-1-foundations.md](phases/phase-1-foundations.md) day by day.

You do **not** need to read the knowledge tree or the resource library first. The daily plan
tells you which lesson, resource and exercise to use, and when.

## How the system works

- **Objectives have IDs** (for example `TON-04`). Lessons, exercises, milestones and the tracker
  all use the same IDs, so you can always see how things connect.
- **Milestones, not videos, prove progress.** You move to the next phase by passing
  practical tests that you record and score with a fixed rubric ([L08](lessons/L08-recording-and-self-assessment.md)).
- **If you fail a test,** the milestone file tells you which underlying weakness to fix and how.
- **Daily sessions** follow one template: warm-up, tone, technique, music, ear/theory, record and log.
- **Spaced review** is built in. Every 7th day is lighter and is used for review.
- **Claims are labelled.** **Fact** means widely agreed; **Recommendation** means this
  curriculum's teaching choice; **Disputed** means sources disagree, with the reasons;
  **Uncertain** means unverified or flute-dependent.

## Honest limits

- **Text cannot hear you or see you.** Use recordings, a tuner and a drone every day. A live
  teacher at the [recommended checkpoints](00-MASTERY-MAP.md#teacher-checkpoints-strongly-recommended)
  will catch problems that no document can.
- **No timeline is guaranteed.** The durations given are typical estimates for 45-60 minutes
  a day. Adjust them to your actual progress.
- **Resources change.** Links were checked on 2026-10-29. See the quality report for what was
  and was not verified.

## Practice health rules

1. Stop if you feel dizzy. Breathe normally and sit down ([L03](lessons/L03-breathing-basics.md)).
2. Stop if you feel pain, numbness or tingling. Rest. Seek medical advice if it persists ([TS-08](troubleshooting/TS-08-body-pain-and-tension.md)).
3. Take a short break every 15-20 minutes.
4. Wipe the flute dry after playing and keep it away from heat and direct sun ([L00](lessons/L00-day-0-your-flute.md)).
