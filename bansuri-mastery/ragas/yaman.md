# Raga Yaman

[Raga index](README.md) | Full lesson: [L21 Raga Yaman](../lessons/L21-raga-yaman.md) | Objective: RAG-03

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Thaat | Kalyan (Yaman is the main raga of this thaat) |
| Notes | S R G M P D N (tivra Ma, everything else shuddha) |
| Jati | Sampurna-sampurna (7 up, 7 down) |
| Aroh | `.N R G M D N S'` (often taught as `.N R G M P D N S'`; Sa and sometimes Pa are skipped going up) |
| Avroh | `S' N D P M G R S` |
| Vadi / samvadi | Ga / Ni |
| Time | Early night. **Disputed:** "first quarter of the night" (Wikipedia), "about 9 pm to midnight" (attributed to The Raga Guide), 19:00-22:00 (ITC SRA) |
| Pakad (common form) | `.N R G R, .N R S, P M G R, .N R S` |

## Character

Serene, devotional, expansive. Phrases often begin from mandra Ni (`.N R G`) rather than from
Sa. Ga and Ni shine.

## Notes to treat carefully

- **Ascent usually starts from `.N`**, skipping Sa: `.N R G`, not `S R G`.
- **Pa** is often skipped in fast ascending lines (`M D N S'`), but used in descent and in
  phrases like `P M G R`.
- **Tivra Ma** (`M`) only. A shuddha `m` turns it into Yaman Kalyan (see below).

## On your C flute

No half-holes, which is why this curriculum teaches Yaman early.

| Note | Fingering | Register | Comment |
|---|---|---|---|
| `.N` | `XXX XOO` | 1 | Seal holes 1-4 firmly |
| `M` | `OOO OOO` | 1 | Can sound weak or breathy; use a slightly narrower, steady air stream and check it with a tuner (F#) |
| `P D N` | `XXX XXX` / `XXX XXO` / `XXX XOO` | 2 | Overblown |

The tricky move is `M` (all open, register 1) to `D` (register 2) in `M D N S'`. Practise
`G M D - | M D N S'` slowly.

## Similar ragas

| Raga | Difference |
|---|---|
| Yaman Kalyan | Adds a little shuddha `m`, usually in `G m G R` |
| Shuddha Kalyan | Bhupali-like ascent (no M, N) |
| Bhupali | Same thaat, but no M and N at all |

## Practice steps

1. Aroh/avroh with drone, 1 note per beat at 60 BPM.
2. Pakad 10 times from memory.
3. Kalyan-scale alankars from [EX-ALK Version C and F-Yaman](../exercises/EX-ALK-alankar-book.md).
4. Swar vistar starting from `.N`, resting on Ga, then on Ni ([L31](../lessons/L31-alap-and-swar-vistar.md)).

**Verification:** Thaat, vadi/samvadi and time checked against [Wikipedia: Yaman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yaman_%28raga%29) on 2026-10-29. The pakad is a widely taught form; teachers vary.
