# Raga Bhimpalasi

[Raga index](README.md) | Objective: RAG-06 | Prerequisite: [L25](../lessons/L25-komal-swaras-and-12-notes.md)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Thaat | Kafi |
| Notes | S R g m P D n |
| Jati | Audav-sampurna (Re and Dha omitted in ascent) |
| Aroh | `.n S g m P n S'` |
| Avroh | `S' n D P m g R S` |
| Vadi / samvadi | Ma / Sa |
| Time | Afternoon. **Disputed:** 12-3 pm, "4th prahar", or 13:00-16:00 depending on the source |
| Characteristic phrases (commonly taught) | `.n S m, m g P m, g m g R S` |

## Character

Yearning, tender, warm. Ma is the centre of gravity. A favourite of bansuri players.

## Notes to treat carefully

- **Ascent skips Re and Dha:** `.n S g m P n S'`.
- **Ma** is the resting note (vadi). Phrases often land on `m`.
- `.n S` (from mandra komal Ni to Sa) is a typical start.

## On your C flute

| Note | Fingering | Register |
|---|---|---|
| `.n` | `XXX XHO` | 1 |
| `g` | `XHO OOO` | 1 |
| `m` | `HOO OOO` | 1 |
| `n` | `XXX XHO` | 2 |

The move `g m` is two neighbouring half-holes: slide from half-hole 2 to half-hole 1 cleanly.
Practise `g m g m` slowly.

## Similar ragas

| Raga | Difference |
|---|---|
| Dhanashri | Same notes; Pa is emphasised instead of Ma |
| Patdeep | Shuddha Ni instead of komal Ni |
| Bageshri | Night raga; Pa used sparingly; `m D n S'` ascent |

## Listening

All recordings: [Listening Library: Bhimpalasi](../08-LISTENING-LIBRARY.md#bhimpalasi).

- **Start with:** R-L100 (Rakesh Chaurasia, Darbar, 10:35).
- **Then:** R-L101 (Rupak Kulkarni, alap only: a model for L31) and R-L102 (Hariprasad Chaurasia, full concert).

Check what you hear against this card: the aroh/avroh, the pakad and the notes to treat carefully.

## Practice steps

1. `.n S g m` and `m P n S'` slowly with drone.
2. Rest on `m` in every phrase for one session.
3. Characteristic phrases from memory.
