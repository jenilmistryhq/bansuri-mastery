# Raga Hamsadhwani

[Raga index](README.md) | Objectives: RAG-08, THY-13

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Thaat | Bilawal |
| Notes | S R G P N (Ma and Dha omitted) |
| Jati | Audav-audav |
| Aroh | `S R G P N S'` |
| Avroh | `S' N P G R S` |
| Vadi / samvadi | **Disputed:** Re / Pa (Wikipedia). Other teaching sources give different pairs; ask your teacher |
| Time | Early night, about 9-12 (Wikipedia) |
| Characteristic phrases | `G P N S'`, `S' N P G`, `G R S .N .P S` (Wikipedia pakad: G P N - S' N P G) |

## Character

Bright and auspicious. Originally a Carnatic raga, adopted into Hindustani music in the 20th
century. Popular for opening pieces.

## Notes to treat carefully

- **Ni** to **Sa'** and **Pa** to **Ni** leaps are the identity of the raga.
- No Ma, no Dha. Be careful not to slip into Bilawal or Yaman phrases.

## On your C flute

No half-holes. Main challenge: `P N S'` in register 2 must be clean and in tune. `N` (`XXX XOO`,
register 2) tends to be weak if holes 1-4 leak.

## Similar ragas

| Raga | Difference |
|---|---|
| Bhupali | Dha instead of Ni |
| Carnatic Hamsadhwani | The original; heavier gamaka ornamentation |

## Listening

All recordings: [Listening Library: Hamsadhwani](../08-LISTENING-LIBRARY.md#hamsadhwani).

- **Start with:** R-L50 (Rakesh Chaurasia and Shashank Subramanyam, 6:47; a Hindustani-Carnatic duet).
- **Then:** R-L51 (Hariprasad Chaurasia, teentaal) for the Hindustani treatment; R-L52 (Pravin Godkhindi).

Check what you hear against this card: the aroh/avroh, the pakad and the notes to treat carefully.

## Practice steps

1. Aroh/avroh with drone.
2. Leap drills `P N P N`, `N S' N S'`.
3. Short improvisation alternating `G P N S'` and `S' N P G`.
