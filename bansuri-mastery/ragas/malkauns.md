# Raga Malkauns

[Raga index](README.md) | Objective: RAG-07 | Prerequisite: all komal notes ([L25](../lessons/L25-komal-swaras-and-12-notes.md)) and PIT-08

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Thaat | Bhairavi (some sources say Asavari) |
| Notes | S g m d n (Re and Pa omitted) |
| Jati | Audav-audav |
| Aroh | `.n S g m d n S'` |
| Avroh | `S' n d m g m g S` (also `S' n d m g S`) |
| Vadi / samvadi | Ma / Sa |
| Time | Late night, 12-3 |
| Pakad (Wikipedia) | `g m d m g m g S` |

## Character

Deep, grave, meditative. One of the oldest and most respected ragas. Slow, heavy movement with
long meends.

## Notes to treat carefully

- **No Pa and no Re.** Pa's absence makes Malkauns feel unanchored and mysterious.
- `m g m g S`: Ma and komal Ga circling, then resting on Sa, is the signature.
- Every note except Sa is a half-hole or vikrit note on your flute. **Intonation is everything.**

## On your C flute

| Note | Fingering | Register |
|---|---|---|
| `.d` / `.n` | `XXX XXH` / `XXX XHO` | 1 |
| `g` | `XHO OOO` | 1 |
| `m` | `HOO OOO` | 1 |
| `d` / `n` | `XXX XXH` / `XXX XHO` | 2 |

This is one of the hardest ragas to play in tune on a C flute with Sa = C. **Bansuri players
often play Malkauns with a different note as Sa** so that fewer half-holes are needed. On your
flute, treat **Ni (B) as Sa**: Malkauns' notes (Sa, +3, +5, +8, +10 semitones) then become
B, D, E, G, A, which are all plain full-hole fingerings (`XXX XOO`, `XXO OOO`, `XOO OOO`,
`XXX XXX`, `XXX XXO`). The trade-off: the drone must be set to B, and your lowest "Sa" is the
mandra B. See [L26](../lessons/L26-keys-transposition-and-limits.md) before attempting this.
(Recommendation: learn it with a teacher.)

## Practice composition (Original, written for this curriculum; not yet reviewed by a teacher)

**Teentaal, 16 beats per line (one cycle), target 50-60 BPM. Starts on sam.** Each `|` marks a vibhag of 4 beats.

```
S - .n S | g m d m | g m g - | S - - - |
g m d n | S' - n d | m g m g | S - - - ||
```

It circles `g m` around the pakad `g m d m g m g S` and has no Re or Pa anywhere. Play it slowly (50-60 BPM) and hold each `g` and `d` long enough to check it against the drone. Practise it with a C drone at 50-60 BPM, one note per beat: sing it first, then play it until every phrase is in tune with the drone, then add a teentaal loop and raise the tempo slowly.

**Uncertain:** this composition was written to follow the rules on this card, but its raga grammar has not been checked by a teacher. Have a teacher check it before you perform it, and prefer a traditional composition from a teacher or from [R-T01 / R-T04](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#5-theory-and-raga-references) when you have one.

## Practice steps

1. Tuner check of all four half-hole notes (+/-15 cents) before any phrase.
2. `g m g S`, `m d n d m`, `S' n d m` slowly, with long tones on each note.
3. Try the "Sa = B" version (drone on B) and compare how easy each version is.
