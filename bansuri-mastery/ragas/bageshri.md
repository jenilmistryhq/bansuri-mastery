# Raga Bageshri

[Raga index](README.md) | Objective: RAG-06 | Prerequisite: [L25](../lessons/L25-komal-swaras-and-12-notes.md)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Thaat | Kafi |
| Notes | S R g m P D n (Pa used sparingly) |
| Aroh | `S g m D n S'` |
| Avroh | `S' n D m P D g m g R S` (variants exist) |
| Vadi / samvadi | Ma / Sa |
| Time | Second part of the night, 9 pm-12 am ("madhya raatri") |
| Pakad (Wikipedia) | `.D .n S, m, m P D, m g R S` |

## Character

Romantic and longing, deep. Ma and Dha are strong. Pa is weak and only appears in specific
phrases like `m P D` or `D m P D`.

## Notes to treat carefully

- **Pa** is not used freely. In ascent, `m D n S'` skips Pa.
- **Shuddha Dha** with **komal Ni**: `D n S'`. Do not make Dha komal.
- `D g m g R S` (a jump down from Dha to komal Ga) is a characteristic descending move.

## On your C flute

| Note | Fingering | Register |
|---|---|---|
| `g` | `XHO OOO` | 1 |
| `m` | `HOO OOO` | 1 |
| `D` | `XXX XXO` | 2 |
| `n` | `XXX XHO` | 2 |
| `.D` / `.n` | `XXX XXO` / `XXX XHO` | 1 |

The jump `m` (half-hole, register 1) to `D` (register 2) is hard. Drill `m D m D` slowly.

## Similar ragas

| Raga | Difference |

## Practice composition (Original, written for this curriculum; not yet reviewed by a teacher)

**Teentaal, 16 beats per line (one cycle), target 60-80 BPM. Starts on sam.** Each `|` marks a vibhag of 4 beats.

```
.D .n S - | m - - - | m P D - | m g R S |
g m D n | S' n D m | P D g m | g R S - ||
```

Line 1 is the pakad `.D .n S, m, m P D, m g R S`; line 2 goes up without Pa (`g m D n S'`) and comes down along the avroh `S' n D m P D g m g R S`. Pa appears only inside `m P D`. Practise it with a C drone at 50-60 BPM, one note per beat: sing it first, then play it until every phrase is in tune with the drone, then add a teentaal loop and raise the tempo slowly.

**Uncertain:** this composition was written to follow the rules on this card, but its raga grammar has not been checked by a teacher. Have a teacher check it before you perform it, and prefer a traditional composition from a teacher or from [R-T01 / R-T04](../02-RESOURCE-LIBRARY.md#5-theory-and-raga-references) when you have one.

## Practice steps

1. `S g m D n S'` with drone, slowly.
2. Pakad from memory.
3. Play Bhimpalasi and Bageshri phrases one after another and listen to the difference.
