# Raga Bageshri

[Raga index](README.md) | Objective: RAG-06 | Prerequisite: [L25](../lessons/L25-komal-swaras-and-12-notes.md)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Thaat | Kafi |
| Notes | S R g m P D n (Pa used sparingly) |
| Aroh | `S g m D n S'` |
| Avroh | `S' n D m P D g m g R S` (variants exist) |
| Vadi / samvadi | Ma / Sa |
| Time | Second part of the night, 9 pm-12 am ("madhya raatri") |
| Pakad (Wikipedia) | `.D .n S, m, m P D, m g R S` |

## Character

Romantic and longing, deep. Ma and Dha are strong. Pa is weak and only appears in specific
phrases like `m P D` or `D m P D`.

## Notes to treat carefully

- **Pa** is not used freely. In ascent, `m D n S'` skips Pa.
- **Shuddha Dha** with **komal Ni**: `D n S'`. Do not make Dha komal.
- `D g m g R S` (a jump down from Dha to komal Ga) is a characteristic descending move.

## On your C flute

| Note | Fingering | Register |
|---|---|---|
| `g` | `XHO OOO` | 1 |
| `m` | `HOO OOO` | 1 |
| `D` | `XXX XXO` | 2 |
| `n` | `XXX XHO` | 2 |
| `.D` / `.n` | `XXX XXO` / `XXX XHO` | 1 |

The jump `m` (half-hole, register 1) to `D` (register 2) is hard. Drill `m D m D` slowly.

## Similar ragas

| Raga | Difference |
|---|---|
| Bhimpalasi | Afternoon; Pa is used freely; ascent `.n S g m P n S'` |
| Rageshri | Shuddha Ga |
| Kafi | Pa strong, plain stepwise movement |

## Listening

All recordings: [Listening Library: Bageshri](../08-LISTENING-LIBRARY.md#bageshri).

- **Start with:** R-L110 (Ronu Majumdar, 5:31).
- **Then:** R-L111 (Ronu Majumdar, live) and R-L112 (Hariprasad Chaurasia, full concert).

Check what you hear against this card: the aroh/avroh, the pakad and the notes to treat carefully.

## Practice steps

1. `S g m D n S'` with drone, slowly.
2. Pakad from memory.
3. Play Bhimpalasi and Bageshri phrases one after another and listen to the difference.
