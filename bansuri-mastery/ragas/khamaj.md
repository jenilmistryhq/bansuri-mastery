# Raga Khamaj

[Raga index](README.md) | Objective: RAG-05 | Prerequisites: [L16](../lessons/L16-shuddha-ma-and-half-holing.md) (m), [L25](../lessons/L25-komal-swaras-and-12-notes.md) (n)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Thaat | Khamaj (the main raga of this thaat) |
| Notes | S R G m P D N n: shuddha Ni going up, komal Ni coming down |
| Jati | Shadav-sampurna (Re omitted in ascent) |
| Aroh | `S G m P D N S'` |
| Avroh | `S' n D P m G R S` |
| Vadi / samvadi | Ga / Ni |
| Time | Night, about 9-12 (Wikipedia). Often heard at any time in light-classical concerts |
| Pakad (Wikipedia) | `n D m P D m G` |

## Character

Romantic, playful, light. The basis of countless thumris, dadras, bhajans and film songs.

## Notes to treat carefully

- **Two Nis:** `N` in ascent (`D N S'`), `n` in descent (`S' n D`). Mixing them up is the most
  common beginner mistake.
- **Re is skipped going up:** `S G m P`, not `S R G m`.
- Light-classical Khamaj often adds other touches; learn the strict form first.

## On your C flute

| Note | Fingering | Register |
|---|---|---|
| `m` | `HOO OOO` | 1 |
| `n` | `XXX XHO` | 2 |
| `N` | `XXX XOO` | 2 |

Drill: `D N S' - | S' n D -` slowly with a tuner until `n` (Bb) and `N` (B) are clearly
different.

## Similar ragas

| Raga | Difference |

## Traditional composition (Bhatkhande, Kramik Pustak Malika)

**What it is:** sargam geet (a composition sung to the note names), teentaal, sthayi (first section) only.

**Source:** V. N. Bhatkhande, *Hindustani Sangeet Paddhati: Kramik Pustak Malika*, Part 1 (Hindi edition, Sangeet Karyalaya, Hathras), raga Khamaj, sargam, page 32. Scans read: [older Hathras printing (eGangotri), leaf n35](https://archive.org/details/skPk_hindustani-sangeet-paddhati-kramik-pustak-malika-1st-book-of-vishnu-narayan-bhar/page/n35/mode/1up) and [2013 Sangeet Karyalaya printing, leaf n32](https://archive.org/details/bhatkhande-hindustani-sangeet-paddhati-kramik-pustak-malika-part-1_compress/page/n32/mode/1up). The two printings agree.

It was transcribed from the public-domain book (Bhatkhande died in 1936) into this course's notation. Check it against the source page before you rely on it.

**Teentaal, 16 beats per line. Starts on sam.** Each `|` marks a vibhag of 4 beats.

```
G G S G | m P G m | n D - m | P D - m |
G - - - | D N S' - | S' n D P | m G R S ||
```

Practise it with a C drone at 50-60 BPM, one note per beat: sing the note names first, then play it until every phrase is in tune with the drone, then add a teentaal loop and raise the tempo slowly.

## Practice steps

1. Two-Ni drill (above), 5 minutes.
2. Aroh/avroh with drone; then the pakad.
3. Find one song or bhajan you know that sounds like Khamaj and check its notes ([L18](../lessons/L18-song-learning-system.md)).
