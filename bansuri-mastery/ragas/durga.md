# Raga Durga

[Raga index](README.md) | Objective: RAG-04 | Prerequisite: shuddha Ma ([L16](../lessons/L16-shuddha-ma-and-half-holing.md))

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Thaat | Bilawal |
| Notes | S R m P D (Ga and Ni are omitted) |
| Jati | Audav-audav |
| Aroh | `S R m P D S'` |
| Avroh | `S' D P m R S` |
| Vadi / samvadi | **Disputed:** Ma / Sa (Wikipedia; Riyaz) vs Pa / Sa (Darbar) |
| Time | Second part of the night, about 9 pm to midnight (Wikipedia) |
| Pakad (Wikipedia, short form) | `R m P D, m R` |
| Characteristic phrases (Wikipedia, longer form) | `R m P D, m P D, m R, S R .D S` |

## Character

Bright, joyful, folk-like. Built on the same shape as Bhupali, but with Ma instead of Ga.

**A useful fact:** Durga uses the same intervals as Bhupali started from Pa. Bhupali's
`P D S' R' G'` (G A C D E) becomes Durga when G is treated as Sa. This is a good first example of
"moving Sa" ([L26](../lessons/L26-keys-transposition-and-limits.md)).

## Notes to treat carefully

- **Shuddha Ma** is central. It must be in tune: check with a tuner (F on your flute).
- Wikipedia notes that **Pa should not be a resting note in descent**.
- `D` to `m` and `m` to `R` are typical leaps and steps. `.D S` is a common cadence.

## On your C flute

| Note | Fingering | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| `m` | `HOO OOO` (half-hole 1) | The only half-hole. Practise `R m R m` until it is steady |
| `P D S'` | register 2 | Overblown |

Because `m` is a half-hole, moving `m` to `P` (half-hole, register 1, to all closed, register 2)
is the hardest move. Drill `m P m P` slowly.

## Similar ragas

| Raga | Difference |
|---|---|
| Bhupali | Ga instead of Ma |
| Durga (Khamaj variant) | A rarer older form; Darbar describes it as almost extinct |
| Malhar-family phrases | `m R P` turns in Malhar ragas can sound similar; Durga has no Ni |

## Listening

All recordings: [Listening Library: Durga](../08-LISTENING-LIBRARY.md#durga).

- **Start with:** R-L30 (Debopriya Chatterjee, 5:09).
- **Then:** R-L31 (Rajendra Prasanna, a lesson) and R-L32 (Hariprasad Chaurasia, full performance).

Check what you hear against this card: the aroh/avroh, the pakad and the notes to treat carefully.

## Practice steps

1. 1 week of `m` half-hole drills ([L16](../lessons/L16-shuddha-ma-and-half-holing.md)) before starting.
2. Aroh/avroh with drone, then the pakad.
3. A 1-minute improvisation resting on Ma and Sa.
