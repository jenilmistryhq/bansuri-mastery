# Raga Kafi

[Raga index](README.md) | Objective: RAG-06 | Prerequisite: [L25](../lessons/L25-komal-swaras-and-12-notes.md) (komal g and n)

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Thaat | Kafi (the main raga of this thaat) |
| Notes | S R g m P D n (komal Ga and komal Ni) |
| Jati | Sampurna-sampurna |
| Aroh | `S R g m P D n S'` |
| Avroh | `S' n D P m g R S` |
| Vadi / samvadi | **Disputed:** Pa / Re (Wikipedia). Many teaching sources give Pa / Sa |
| Time | Wikipedia: second part of the night (9 pm-12 am). In folk and light music (Holi songs, for example) it is played at many times |
| Pakad (Wikipedia) | `S R g m P D n S', S' n D P m g R S` |
| Typical opening phrase (Wikipedia) | `m P D P m g R, g R S, .n .D S` |

## Character

Folksy, gentle, spring-like. Close to the Western Dorian mode. Used widely in folk songs,
bhajans and Holi songs.

## Notes to treat carefully

- `g` and `n` must be really komal (Eb and Bb on your flute).
- Kafi in light music sometimes uses shuddha G or N as colour. Learn the strict form first.

## On your C flute

| Note | Fingering | Register |
|---|---|---|
| `g` | `XHO OOO` | 1 |
| `m` | `HOO OOO` | 1 |
| `n` | `XXX XHO` | 2 |
| `.n` | `XXX XHO` | 1 |

Three half-holes in one scale. Drill `R g m P` and `D n S'` slowly with a tuner before playing
full phrases.

## Similar ragas

| Raga | Difference |
|---|---|
| Bageshri | Same thaat; Pa weak, Ma very strong, distinctive `D n S' ... m g R S` movement |
| Bhimpalasi | Same thaat; Re and Dha are skipped in ascent; afternoon raga |
| Shivranjani | Only S R g P D |

## Listening

All recordings: [Listening Library: Kafi](../08-LISTENING-LIBRARY.md#kafi).

- **Start with:** R-L90 (Ronu Majumdar, 7:47).
- **Then:** R-L92 (Hariprasad Chaurasia, short) and R-L93 (Rakesh Chaurasia, dhun in Deepchandi tala).
- Most bansuri Kafi recordings are light-classical (dhun). That is authentic for this raga.

Check what you hear against this card: the aroh/avroh, the pakad and the notes to treat carefully.

## Practice steps

1. Half-hole drill with tuner, 5 minutes: `g` and `n` each held 4 s within +/-20 cents.
2. Aroh/avroh with drone.
3. The typical opening phrase, then free slow playing.
