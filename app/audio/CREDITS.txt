Both are amateur recordings at low bitrate (22 kHz, about 50 kbps). They were checked for
licence and format, not by ear. They let a beginner hear the real instrument's breathy tone;
for professional tone, use the recordings in bansuri-mastery/08-LISTENING-LIBRARY.md.
